Passage 8

扫码跟读

Not all great writers are great spellers .If you want to be published , it's vital to submit a perfect, professionally presented manuscript ( 原稿 ).That means good grammar and no spelling mistakes.No editor is likely to tolerate a writer who does not take the trouble to spell words correctly .

I keep two reference books close by on my desk : a dictionary and a thesaurus ( 同义词词典 ).I don't trust my laptop ’s spellchecker.It takes no account of the context .Of course, these days there are plenty of online dictionaries and thesauruses, but I'm old-fashioned enough to prefer a hard cover and pages I can leaf through with my fingers .I use the Concise Oxford Dictionary and the Collins Thesaurus .

Of course, a dictionary is not only for spelling.It should give you a precise definition of each word, thus differentiating it from other words whose meanings are similar , but not identical .It will also usually show how the word is pronounced .

In addition , I have an old two-volume copy of the Shorter Oxford Dictionary , picked up a few years ago in a bookshop sale for just 99 pence .Of course, with its 2,672 pages, it's not exactly short.It contains around 163,000 words, plus word combinations and idiomatic phrases .I don't often use this dictionary.However, if I need to check the origin of a word or to look up examples of its usage , there's nothing better.

For well over a hundred years the most influential English dictionary was Samuel Johnson's A Dictionary of the English Language published in 1755.“To make dictionaries is dull work,”wrote Johnson, illustrating one definition of “dull”.But a dictionary can be a pleasure to read.A few minutes spent casting your eye over a page or two can be a rewarding experience .

并非所有伟大的作家都是 拼写高手 。如果你想 发表 作品，提交一份完美且呈现得 专业的 原稿 至关重要 。这 意味着 要 语法 正确，没有 拼写 错误。没有哪个 编辑 会容忍一个不愿花心思把单词 拼写正确 的作者。

我 书桌 上常放着两本 参考 书：一本 词典 和一本同义词词典。我不相信 笔记本电脑 的拼写检查程序，它不 考虑上下文 。当然，现在有 很多 线上的词典和同义词词典，但我比较守旧，更喜欢传统的硬 皮 书和可以用 手指翻 阅的书页。我使用的是《牛津简明词典》和《柯林斯同义词词典》。

当然，词典不仅仅是用来查阅拼写的。它可以给出每个单词的 精确 含义， 从而 将其与其他含义 相似但 不 相同的 单词 区分 开来。它通常还会 显示 单词的 发音 。

此外 ，我还有一套老版的两卷本《牛津词典（简编本）》，是几年 前 在 书店促销 时买的，只花了99 便士 。当然，2672页的篇幅并不算短。它包含 约 163000个单词， 以及 词 组 和惯用 短语 。我并不常用这本词典。不过，如果我需要 查 一个单词的 来源 或 找 这个单词的 用法示例 时，就没有比它更好的了。
