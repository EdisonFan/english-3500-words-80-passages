### 课文一 · Reading and Thinking：FIRST IMPRESSIONS（第一印象）

> **7:00 a.m.**
> So this is it — senior high school at last! I'm not outgoing so I'm a little anxious right now. I want to make a good first impression. Will I make any friends? What if no one talks to me?
>
> **12:30 p.m.**
> I just had my first maths class at senior high school! The class was difficult, but the teacher was kind and friendly. He even told us a funny story and everyone laughed so much! I found most of my classmates and teachers friendly and helpful.
>
> **5:32 p.m.**
> This afternoon, we had our chemistry class in the science lab. The lab is new and the lesson was great, but the guy next to me tried to talk to me the whole time. I couldn't concentrate on the experiment. I really wanted to tell him to please be quiet and leave me alone!
>
> **10:29 p.m.**
> What a day! This morning, I was worried that no one would talk to me. But I was wrong. I didn't feel awkward or frightened at all. I miss my friends from junior high school, but I believe I will make new friends here and there's a lot to explore at senior high. I feel much more confident than I felt this morning. I think that tomorrow will be a great day!

**中文翻译**

> **上午 7:00**：终于，就这样开始了——高中生活！我性格不外向，所以现在有点儿焦虑。我想给人留下好的第一印象。我会交到朋友吗？要是没人跟我说话怎么办？
>
> **下午 12:30**：我刚刚上了高中的第一节数学课！这堂课很难，但老师很和蔼、友好。他甚至给我们讲了一个有趣的故事，大家都笑得前仰后合！我发现我的大多数同学和老师都很友好、乐于助人。
>
> **下午 5:32**：今天下午，我们在科学实验室上了化学课。实验室是新的，课也很棒，但坐在我旁边的那个家伙一直试图跟我说话。我无法集中精力做实验。我真想让他安静点，别来打扰我！
>
> **晚上 10:29**：多好的一天啊！今天早上我还担心没人会跟我说话。但我错了。我一点儿也不觉得尴尬、害怕了。我想念初中的朋友们，但我相信我在这里会交到新朋友，而且高中有很多值得探索的东西。我感觉比今天早上自信多了。我想明天会是美好的一天！

### 课文一 · Reading and Thinking：THE FRESHMAN CHALLENGE（新生的挑战）

> Hi! My name is Adam and I'm a freshman at senior high school. Going from junior high school to senior high school is a really big challenge. The first week was a little confusing.
>
> First, I had to think very carefully about which courses I wanted to take. The school adviser helped me choose the suitable ones: maths, English, chemistry, world history, and Chinese. I know that Chinese is a very difficult language, but I hope to be fluent when I graduate. My adviser recommended that I should sign up for advanced literature because I like English and I'm good at it.
>
> I had to choose extra-curricular activities, too. I tried to join the school football team, but the coach told me that I didn't play well enough. Obviously, I was unhappy, but I won't quit. I'll find a way to improve on my own so that I can make the team next year. I joined a volunteer club instead. Every Wednesday, we work at a soup kitchen and hand out food to homeless people in the community.
>
> I know I'll have to study harder as a senior high school student and get used to being responsible for a lot more. I'm a bit worried about keeping up with the other students in my advanced course, and it'll be quite difficult to get used to all the homework. Still, I'm happy to be here. Studying hard isn't always fun, but I'll be well prepared for university or whatever else comes in the future.
>
> 10 September 2018

**中文翻译**

> 嗨！我叫亚当，是高中一年级的新生。从初中过渡到高中真是一项巨大的挑战。第一周有点让人困惑。
>
> 首先，我必须非常认真地考虑我想选哪些课程。学校的指导老师帮我选了合适的：数学、英语、化学、世界史和中文。我知道中文是一门非常难学的语言，但我希望毕业时能说得很流利。因为我喜欢英语而且成绩不错，指导老师建议我选修高级文学。
>
> 我还得选择课外活动。我试图加入学校足球队，但教练说我踢得不够好。显然，我很不开心，但我不会放弃。我会自己想办法提高，这样明年就能入选球队了。于是我改参加了一个志愿者俱乐部。每个星期三，我们在施舍处干活，把食物分发给社区里无家可归的人。
>
> 我知道，作为一名高中生，我必须更努力地学习，并习惯对更多的事情负责。我有点担心在高级课程中跟不上其他同学，而且适应所有的作业会相当困难。不过，我还是很高兴来到这里。努力学习并不总是有趣的，但我会为将来上大学或任何其他事情做好充分准备。
>
> 2018 年 9 月 10 日

### 课文二 · Reading for Writing：Letter of Advice（建议信范文）

> **Dear Worried Friend,**
> You wrote that you are very worried about your friend, Chen Lei. I understand quite well that you are anxious and feel terrible. You think that your friend plays computer games too often and spends too much time online.
> I recommend that you talk to your friend about his behaviour. It is not unusual for teenagers of your generation to be attracted to computer games and the online world. But spending too much time online is unhealthy and makes it very difficult to focus on other things in life. Some students even become addicted to the Internet and cannot concentrate on school and family life. I think you should encourage your friend to try new hobbies. Why not discuss the problem together? I am sure he will listen to you, since you are his good friend.
> **All the best, Susan Luo**

**中文翻译**

> **亲爱的担忧的朋友：** 你来信说你非常担心你的朋友陈雷。我十分理解你的焦虑和难过。你认为你的朋友玩电脑游戏太频繁，并且在网上花的时间太多。
> 我建议你和你的朋友谈谈他的行为。对于你们这一代的青少年来说，被电脑游戏和网络世界所吸引并不罕见。但是花太多时间上网是不健康的，而且会让人很难集中精力于生活中的其他事情。有些学生甚至沉迷于网络，无法专注于学校和家庭生活。我认为你应该鼓励你的朋友去尝试新的爱好。为什么不一起讨论这个问题呢？我相信他会听你的，因为你是他的好朋友。
> **祝好，苏珊·罗**

### 课文一 · Reading and Thinking：TRAVEL PERU（秘鲁旅行）

> **PERU**
> Peru is a country on the Pacific coast of South America with three main areas: narrow, dry, flat land running along the coast, the Andes Mountains, and the Amazon rainforest. In the 1400s and 1500s, Peru was the centre of the powerful ancient Inca Empire. The Inca emperor lived in the now-famous site Machu Picchu. Spain took control of Peru in the 16th century and ruled until 1821. It is for this reason that Spanish is the main official language of Peru.
>
> **Amazon Rainforest Tour**
> A short flight from Cusco takes you from the Andes into the Amazon rainforest. From there, you'll spend one day travelling by boat to your accommodation in the middle of the forest. You can then spend three days exploring the rainforest with a local guide and enjoying the plants and animals unique to the rainforest.
>
> **Machu Picchu Tour**
> This four-day walking tour will take you on amazing paths through the Andes Mountains on the way to the city of Machu Picchu. ### 课文二 · Reading for Writing：THE UNDERDOG（弱者）

> Paul and I were on our school basketball team, The Lions. Paul's favourite player was Tyrone Bogues, a guy who played for the Charlotte Hornets. Bogues was only 1.6 metres tall, which made him the shortest player ever in the NBA. Guess what? Paul was only 1.6 metres tall too. That's the fatal weakness for a basketball player.
> The coach was not sure about Paul. Thus he was usually on the bench, being just a replacement. But Paul did not give up. He worked really hard and spent hours practising. He had a strong desire to play for the team.
> Then came the big game against The Bears — our main competitors, a team whose record that season had been perfect. They hadn't lost a single game. It would be a tough one.
> During the game, one of our players got hurt and could not play. "Give him a shot, coach!" someone shouted. "He can't play!" the coach replied.
> "Let me try, coach! I won't let you down!" Paul cried.
> "OK, OK!" the coach finally agreed.
> Paul jumped up and rushed onto the court. "Don't let us down, Paul. It's your time to shine," the coach said.
> Paul made shot after shot. In the end, our team won by two points.
> "Well," said the coach as he hit Paul on the shoulder, "you've just earned your place on the team, big guy!"

**中文翻译**

> 保罗和我是学校狮子篮球队的队员。保罗最喜欢的球员是特里·博格斯（Tyrone Bogues），一个效力于夏洛特黄蜂队的家伙。博格斯只有 1.6 米高，这使他成为 NBA 史上最矮的球员。你猜怎么着？保罗也只有 1.6 米高。对篮球运动员来说，那是致命的弱点。
> 教练对保罗并不看好。因此他通常坐在替补席上，只是个替补队员。但保罗没有放弃。他非常努力地训练，花大量时间练习。他有着为球队出战的强烈渴望。
> 接着，与熊队（The Bears）的大赛到来了——那是我们主要的竞争对手，那支球队那个赛季至今保持全胜。他们一场未输。这将是一场硬仗。
> 比赛进行中，我们的一名队员受伤，无法继续比赛。"让他试试吧，教练！"有人喊道。"他不能上场！"教练回答。
> "让我试试吧，教练！我不会让您失望的！"保罗喊道。
> "好吧，好吧！"教练终于同意了。
> 保罗跳起来冲上球场。"别让我们失望，保罗。该你发光了。"教练说。
> 保罗一个接一个地投篮命中。最终，我们队以两分优势获胜。
> "嗯，"教练拍着保罗的肩膀说，"你刚刚为自己在队里赢得了位置，大个子！"

### 课文一 · Reading and Thinking：THE CHINESE WRITING SYSTEM: CONNECTING THE PAST AND THE PRESENT（汉语书写体系：连接过去与现在）

> China is widely known for its ancient civilisation which has continued all the way through into modern times, despite the many ups and downs in its history. There are many reasons why this has been possible, but one of the main factors has been the Chinese writing system.
> At the beginning, written Chinese was a picture-based language. It dates back several thousand years to the use of *longgu* — animal bones and shells on which symbols were carved by ancient Chinese people. Some of the ancient symbols can still be seen in today's *hanzi*.
> By the Shang Dynasty (around 1600–1046 BCE), these symbols had become a well-developed writing system. Over the years, the system developed into different forms, as it was a time when people were divided geographically, leading to many varieties of dialects and characters. This, however, changed under Emperor Qinshihuang of the Qin Dynasty (221–207 BCE).
> Emperor Qinshihuang united the seven major states into one unified country where the Chinese writing system began to develop in one direction. That writing system was of great importance in uniting the Chinese people and culture. Even today, no matter where Chinese people live or what dialect they speak, they can all still communicate in writing.
> Written Chinese has also become an important means by which China's present is connected with its past. People in modern times can read the classic works which were written by Chinese in ancient times. The high regard for the Chinese writing system can be seen in the development of Chinese characters as an art form, known as Chinese calligraphy, which has become an important part of Chinese culture.
> Today, the Chinese writing system is still an important part of Chinese culture. As China plays a greater role in global affairs, an increasing number of international students are beginning to appreciate China's culture and history through this amazing language.

**中文翻译**

> 中国，因其一路绵延至今的古代文明而著称于世，尽管历史跌宕起伏（依然如此）。"这种情况之所以可能出现"的原因有很多，但主要因素之一就是汉语书写体系。
> 起初，书面汉字是一种基于图形的语言。它可以追溯至数千年前对"龙骨"（*longgu*）的使用——就是古代中国人刻下符号的动物骨头和甲壳。这些古代符号中的一部分在今天的汉字中依然可以看到。
> 到了商朝（大约公元前 1600—1046 年），这些符号已经演变为一套成熟完善的书写体系。经过了好些年，这套体系发展成了不同的形式，乃是由于当时人们在地理上是分隔开的，就导致方言和字体产生出了不同种类。这种情况却在秦朝（公元前 221—207 年）秦始皇治下发生了变化。
> 秦始皇将七个主要城邦统一成了一个整齐划一的国家，其中汉语书写体系开始朝着一个方向发展。这个书写体系对于中国人民和文化的统一有着巨大的重要性。即使在今天，无论中国人住在哪里、说着何种方言，都还能以书写的方式进行交流。
> 书面汉语，也已成为中国连接现在与过去的重要方式。现代中国人可以读懂古代中国人写的经典作品。对汉语书写体系的高度推崇，可以从汉字作为一种艺术形式的发展中看到；这被称作中国书法艺术，而它已经成为中国文化的一个重要组成部分。
> 今天，汉语书写体系依然是中国文化的重要组成部分。随着中国在全球事务中扮演的角色变得更为重要，越来越多的国际学生开始透过这种神奇的语言欣赏中国文化和历史。
