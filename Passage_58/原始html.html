Goffin's cockatoos, native to Australasia, are a kind of small parrot that has a white body with hints of yellow and orange .They mimic sounds and dance to music, which often amuses their owners.The cockatoos have been shown to have similar shape-recognition abilities to a human two-year-old.Though not known to use tools in the wild , the birds have proved skillful at tool use while being kept in cages .In a recent experiment, cockatoos were presented with a box with a nut inside it.The clear front of the box had a“ keyhole ”in a geometric shape, and the birds were given five differently shaped “keys” to choose from. Inserting the correct “key” would let out the nut.

In humans, babies can put a round shape in a round hole from around one year of age.They are quite gifted in their vision and can make subtle discriminations between different shapes.But it will be another year before they are able to do the same with less symmetrical （对称的） shapes.This ability to recognize that a shape will need to be turned in a specific direction to fit within the boundary of the puzzle is called an “allocentric frame of reference”.It is linked to understanding geometry .Developing this skill helps in activities like reading a map or navigating new environments.In the experiment, Goffin's cockatoos were able to select the right tool for the job, in most cases, by visual recognition alone.Where trial-and-error was used, the cockatoos did better than monkeys in similar tests.This indicates that Goffin's cockatoos do indeed possess an allocentric frame of reference when moving objects in space, implying that these birds have cognitive abilities comparable to those of two-year-old babies.The comparison has astonished researchers who had underestimated the extent to which animal intelligence can develop.

The next step, according to the researchers, is to try and work out whether the cockatoos rely entirely on visual clues , or also use a sense of touch in making their shape selections .Findings of future studies should represent significant progress in the field of animal cognition and may lead to innovations in how we appreciate animal minds.

戈芬凤头鹦鹉 原产 于澳大拉西亚，是一种小型 鹦鹉 ，身体呈白色，有少量黄色和 橙色 羽毛点缀。它们会 模仿 声音，还能随着音乐 起舞 ，常常 逗乐 它们的主人。经验证，凤头鹦鹉的形状识别能力与人类两岁的儿童相近。尽管人们不知道这种 鸟 在 野外 会使用工具，但它们被关在 笼子 里时却已证明自己在使用工具方面很 熟练 。在最近的一项实验中，研究人员给凤头鹦鹉展示了一个装有 坚果 的盒子。盒子透明的正面有一个 几何 形状的“ 钥匙孔 ”，研究人员给这些鸟儿五把不同 形状 的“钥匙”供它们选择。 插入正确的 “钥匙”就能让坚果掉出来。

对于人类来说，婴儿从一岁左右起就能把圆形放入圆孔中。他们在 视觉 方面极具 天赋 ，能够对不同的形状做出 细微的区分 。但要再过一年，他们才能对不太对称的形状做到同样的操作。这种认识到一个形状需要朝特定方向转动才能与拼图的 边界 相契合的能力被称为“异我中心参照系”。它与理解 几何图形 相关。培养这种能力有助于看 地图 或在新环境中辨别方向等活动。在实验中，戈芬凤头鹦鹉在大多数情况下仅通过视觉 识别 就能选取合适的工具。在使用“试错法”的情况下，凤头鹦鹉在类似测试中的表现要优于 猴子 。这 表明 ，在空间中移动 物体 时，戈芬凤头鹦鹉确实 拥有 异我中心参照系，这 意味着 ，这种鸟类具有与两岁婴儿 相当 的认知能力。这一 对比 结果让研究人员感到 震惊 ，因为他们此前低估了动物智力发展的 程度 。
