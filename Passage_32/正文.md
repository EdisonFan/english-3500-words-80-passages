# Passage 32

Nowadays, in many places around the world, kids play soccer less than before. One cause is that many kids are busy with their studies. Besides, many parents don't know soccer's real benefits to kids. In fact, soccer offers a wide range of physical, social, and emotional benefits, whether played in a stadium, a school yard or on a square. Here are some reasons why kids should play the beautiful game.

Soccer is a team sport.

Teamwork is a life skill that kids need to learn. A soccer team is like a well-organized package of players, each with specific roles and responsibilities. Being part of a team isn't just about learning how to follow strict rules and share talents; it's also about teaching kids the importance of order and how to face failure. It doesn't end there, either. By learning to rely on one another, children develop empathy and respect for their teammates, which are critical components of good cooperation.

Soccer shapes motor skills.

Nowadays, many kids prefer to stay in their rooms to have fun through smartphones. So they have less time outdoors for sports. Soccer is an excellent form of PE because it engages almost every muscle group. By playing youth soccer on the court, kids get a chance to develop their physical strength and motor skills.

Soccer plays a part in school performance.

Soccer can improve students' concentration in the classroom. Besides, soccer is a fast-paced game, so kids can learn how to solve problems quickly. Plus, soccer is a sport which involves getting the ball from point A to point B. So kids' visual-spatial ability can be improved when they play the game, which can help them learn subjects like Physics.

Soccer improves the personal character.

The World Health Organization recommends kids get at least 60 minutes of exercise daily. Winning a game or simply improving skills can give children a sense of accomplishment. When a child has to kick the ball past an opponent or tackle them, they also learn perseverance and resilience. Usually, kids who like sports such as soccer are more outgoing. They are better able to handle stress or panic in their lives and less likely to fall into negative habits like abuse of alcohol or drugs.
