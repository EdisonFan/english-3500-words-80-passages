Nowadays , in many places around the world, kids play soccer less than before.One cause is that many kids are busy with their studies.Besides, many parents don't know soccer's real benefits to kids.In fact, soccer offers a wide range of physical, social, and emotional benefits, whether played in a stadium , a school yard or on a square .Here are some reasons why kids should play the beautiful game .

Soccer is a team sport .

Teamwork is a life skill that kids need to learn.A soccer team is like a well-organized ( 组织良好的 ) package of players , each with specific roles and responsibilities.Being part of a team isn't just about learning how to follow strict rules and share talents ; it's also about teaching kids the importance of order and how to face failure.It doesn't end there , either.By learning to rely on one another, children develop empathy and respect for their teammates, which are critical components of good cooperation.

Soccer shapes motor skills.

Nowadays, many kids prefer to stay in their rooms to have fun through smartphones .So they have less time outdoors for sports.Soccer is an excellent form of PE because it engages almost every muscle group.By playing youth soccer on the court , kids get a chance to develop their physical strength and motor skills.

Soccer plays a part in school performance .

Soccer can improve students' concentration in the classroom.Besides, soccer is a fast-paced game, so kids can learn how to solve problems quickly.Plus, soccer is a sport which involves getting the ball from point A to point B.So kids' visual-spatial ( 视觉空间 ) ability can be improved when they play the game, which can help them learn subjects like Physics .

Soccer improves the personal character.

The World Health Organization recommends kids get at least 60 minutes of exercise daily. Winning a game or simply improving skills can give children a sense of accomplishment.When a child has to kick the ball past an opponent or tackle them, they also learn perseverance ( 坚持不懈 ) and resilience ( 适应力 ). Usually , kids who like sports such as soccer are more outgoing .They are better able to handle stress or panic in their lives and less likely to fall into negative habits like abuse of alcohol or drugs.

短文译文

如今 ，在世界许多地方，孩子们踢 足球 的时间都比以前少了。其中一个 原因 是很多孩子 忙 于学业。此外，很多家长并不了解足球对孩子的 真正 益处。事实上，无论是在 体育场 、学校 操场 还是 广场 上踢球，足球都能给孩子们带来身体、社交和情感等多方面的益处。以下是孩子们应该参与这项美好 运动 的一些原因。

足球是一项团队 运动 。

团队合作 是孩子们需要学习的一项生活技能。一支足球队就像一个组织良好的 球员组合 ，每个球员都有特定的角色和职责。成为团队的一员不仅仅是学习如何遵守 严格的 规则并分享个人 才能 ，还在于教会孩子们 秩序 的重要性，以及如何面对失败。而且，还不止于 此 。通过学习相互依靠，孩子们会对队友产生 同理心 和尊重，这些都是良好合作的关键要素。

足球塑造 运动 技能。

如今，很多孩子更喜欢待在房间里，通过 智能手机娱乐 。因此，他们 在户外 运动的时间变少了。足球是一种 极佳的体育 运动形式，因为它几乎能锻炼到每一个肌肉群。通过在 球场 上参加青少年足球活动，孩子们有机会培养他们的 体 能和运动技能。

足球对学业 表现 有影响。

足球可以提高学生在课堂上的 注意力 。此外，足球是一项节奏很快的运动，所以孩子们可以学会如何快速 解决 问题。再者，足球是一项需要把球从A点传到B点的运动。因此，孩子们在踢球时可以提高视觉空间能力，这有助于他们学习 物理 等科目。

足球改善个人品格。

世界卫生 组织建议 儿童每天至少进行60分钟的运动。 赢得 比赛或仅仅是技能提升都能给孩子们带来成就感。当一个孩子必须将球 踢 过 对手 或 进行抢断 时，他们也学会了坚持不懈和坚韧不拔。 通常 ，喜欢足球等运动的孩子都更加 外向 。他们能更好地应对生活中的压力或 恐慌 ，而且不太可能染上 酗酒 或吸毒等 不良 的习惯。
