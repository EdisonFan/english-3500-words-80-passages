Can a small group of drones ( 无人机 ) guarantee the safety and reliability of railways and, at the same time, help railway operators save billions of euros each year? That is the very likely future of applying today's “eyes in the sky” technology to make sure that more than one million kilometers of rail tracks and infrastructure ( 基础设施 ) worldwide are safe for trains 24/7.

Drones are already being used to examine high-tension electrical lines.They could do precisely the same thing to inspect railway lines and other vital aspects of rail infrastructure, such as the correct position of railway tracks and the functioning of switching points.The more regularly they can be inspected, the more railway safety, reliability and on-time performance will be improved.Costs would be cut and operations would be more efficient across the board.

That includes huge savings in maintenance costs and better protection of railway personnel's safety.It is calculated that European railways alone spend over 50 billion euros a year on maintenance, including sending maintenance staff to inspect and repair the rail infrastructure.That can be dangerous work, especially in deserts and snowy mountainous regions, which could be avoided with drones assisting the crews’ efforts.

Railway officials are recognizing the importance of drones in defense against potential accidents.By using the latest technologies, drones could detect faults in the rail or switches, before they cause any safety problems.For example, drones could provide real-time ( 实时的 ) alerts to approaching trains, when kangaroos or other wildlife wander onto the tracks in Australia.In regions with active volcanoes , drones can monitor their activities near railway lines, ensuring that villages and rail services are safe in case they erupt .

Engineers are now working on a new concept: the rail drones of the future.They will be moving on the track ahead of the train, and programmed to operate autonomously .Tiny drones with advanced sensors and AI travelling ahead of the train could guide it like a co-pilot .With their ability to see ahead, they could signal any problem, so that fast-moving trains would be able to react in time.In case of emergencies, drones could also assist in working with ambulance services.Additionally, the huge volume of data collected by drones can be analyzed to predict repair needs, benefiting both the repair division and insurance providers.As for the legal aspects, consultation with lawyers would be required to address the challenges of drone use.

无人机已经被用于检查 高压电 线。在 检查 铁路线路和基础设施的其他关键部分（如铁轨的位置是否正确、道岔的状态是否良好）时，无人机也能发挥同样的作用。 定期 检查越频繁，铁路的安全性、可靠性和准点率就会越高。成本会降低， 运营 效率也会全面提高。
