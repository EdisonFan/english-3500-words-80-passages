扫码跟读

Imagine a child standing on a diving board four feet high and asking himself the question:“Should I jump?”This is what motivation or the lack of it can do.Motivation and goal setting are the two sides of the same coin .Without motivation, you can neither set a goal nor reach it.Like the child on the diving board, you will stay undecided .

So how should you motivate yourself? How should you stay motivated to achieve the goal? First, you need to evaluate yourself-your values, your strengths , your weaknesses, your achievements, your desires , etc.Only then should you realize your goals.Just as a camel endures the harsh conditions of the desert, people have their respective abilities to help them persevere ( 坚持 ).

You also need to judge the quality and depth of your motivation.This is quite important, because it is directly related to your commitment .There are times when your heart is not in your work.This can affect your work.So, slow down and think about what you really want to do at that moment .Clarity of thoughts can help you move forward .

Another way of setting realistic goals is to analyze your short and long-term objectives , keeping in mind your beliefs, values and strengths.Even running race winners need to run lap after lap to build endurance ( 耐久力 ).Instead of trying to lose thirty kilos in a month, aim to lose a kilo per week.Don't aim to climb over a fence that's too high without preparation.Remember that goals are flexible .They can change according to circumstances .They also need to be measurable .

Your personal circumstances are equally important.For example, you may want to be a pilot but can't become one because your eyesight is not good enough.However, this should not discourage you.You should reassess your goals, and motivate yourself to set a new goal.

You will surely need to overcome some difficulties, some planned, but most unplanned.You will easily throw in the towel without ample motivation.Make sure that you plan for these difficulties.Don't turn a deaf ear to advice.By working with someone else, you can double your efforts and sail more smoothly towards your goals.

想象 一下，一个孩子站在 四英尺 高的 跳 板上，问自己这个问题：“我应该跳吗？”这是 动力 或缺乏动力带来的情形。动力和目标设定是同一枚 硬币 的正反两 面 。没有动力，你 既不 能设定目标，也无法 实现 目标。就像跳板上的这个孩子一样，你会 犹豫不决 。

那么，你应该如何激励自己呢？你应该如何保持动力来 实现 目标呢？首先，你需要 评估 自己，包括你的价值观、 优势 、劣势、成就、 愿望 等。只有这样，你才能实现你的目标。就像 骆驼 能够 忍受 沙漠的恶劣环境一样，每个人都有 各自的 能力来帮助自己坚持下去。

你还需要判断动力的 质量 和 深度 。这一点 非常 重要，因为它与你的 投入直接 相关。有时候你的心思不在工作上，这就会 影响 你的工作。所以， 慢 下来，想想你那个 时刻 真正想做什么。清晰的 思路 可以帮助你 向前 迈进。
