Rainforests are home to a rich variety of life and are some of the places with the greatest botanical diversity on Earth.Can you believe that a single bush in the Amazon may have more species of ants than the whole of Britain! Some species are also considered living descendants ( 后代 ) of ancient dinosaurs .About 480 species of trees may be found in just one hectare of rainforest.

Rainforests are the lungs of the planet—storing vast quantities of carbon dioxide and producing a significant amount of the world's oxygen.Rainforests have their own perfect system for ensuring their own survival .The tall trees can reach heights of over a hundred meters , creating a canopy ( 树冠层 ) that is often enveloped in foggy mist .The canopy of branches and leaves protects itself, smaller plants, and the forest animals from heavy rain , intense dry heat from the sun and strong winds .

Amazingly , the trees grow in such a way that their leaves and branches, although close together, never actually touch those of another tree.Scientists think this is the plants' way of preventing the spread of any tree diseases and making life more difficult for leaf-eating insects like caterpillars .To survive in the forest, animals must climb, jump or fly across the gaps .The ground floor of the forest is rich with mushrooms and other fungi ( 真菌 ). Silent predators like tigers move through this realm ( 领域 ).It is not all tangled leaves and bushes, like in films, but is actually fairly clear.It is where leaves turn into food for the trees and other forest life.

They are not called rainforests for nothing! Rainforests can generate 75% of their own rain.At least 80 inches of rain a year is normal—and in some areas there may be as much as 430 inches of rain annually .This is real rain—your umbrella may protect you in a shower , but it won't keep you dry if there is a full rainstorm .In just two hours, streams can rise ten to twenty feet.The air is filled with the buzz ( 嗡嗡声 ) of insects, including mosquitoes , which thrive in rainy and wet conditions.The humidity ( 湿气 ) of large rainforests contributes to the formation of rainclouds that may travel to other countries in need of rain.

Today, rainforests are under threat from human activities such as illegal logging .The wounds caused to these forests have widespread consequences.We must take steps to conserve these precious environments.

热带雨林 是 丰富 多样的生物的栖息地，也是地球上 植物 多样性最为丰富的地方之一。你能相信吗？亚马孙雨林的一丛灌木中的 蚂蚁 种类可能比整个英国的蚂蚁种类还要多！有些物种还被认为是远古 恐龙 的现存后代。在仅仅一 公顷 的雨林中，就可以发现大约480种 树木 。

热带雨林是“地球之肺”——储存着 大 量的二氧化碳，并为全球产出大量的氧气。热带雨林拥有一套完美的系统，可以保证自身的 存续 。 高大的 树木可以长到超过一百 米 的高度，形成一个常常被 雾气笼罩 的树冠层。枝叶构成的树冠层保护着树木自身、低矮的植物以及 森林 中的动物，使其免受 大雨 、烈日的酷 热 以及强 风 的侵袭。

令人惊奇的是 ，这些树木的生长方式使得它们的叶子和枝条尽管彼此靠近，但从不真正地触碰到另一棵树的叶子和枝条。科学家们认为，这是植物 防止 树木间的疾病传播，并让 毛毛虫 等食叶昆虫更难生存的一种方式。为了在森林中 生存 ，动物们必须攀爬、跳跃或 飞 越这些 间隙 。森林的地面层到处都是 蘑菇 和其他真菌。 老虎 等 安静的 掠食者在这个领域中穿行。它并不像电影中那样全是杂乱 缠绕的 树叶和灌木丛，实际上相当开阔。这里是树叶转化为树木和其他森林生物所需养料的地方。
