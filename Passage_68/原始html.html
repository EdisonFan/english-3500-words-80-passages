Color is fundamental in home design—something you'll always have in every room.A grasp of how to manage color in your spaces is one of the first steps to creating rooms that reflect your preference .Do you want a room that's full of life? Professional ? Or are you just looking for a place to relax after a long day? A bright yellow accent wall can make a room feel livelier . Blue tones are often associated with calmness.An elegant combination of colors can transform a simple room into a special space.

Over the years, there have been a number of different techniques to help designers approach this important point.Many people feel confused about the endless color shades and mixes available.The difficulty lies in choosing colors that match your personal taste while also complementing the existing components of your room.An unsuitable color can even disturb the overall harmony.But the good news is that there are really only three kinds of decisions you need to make about color in your home: the small ones, the medium ones, and the large ones.

Small color choices are the ones we're most familiar with.They're the little spots of color like throw pillows , mirrors and baskets that most of us use to decorate our rooms.Adding fabric and metal elements through small color choices gives depth and interest to the space.Less tiring than painting your walls and less expensive than buying a colorful sofa , small color choices bring with them the significant benefit of being easily changeable .

Medium color choices are generally furniture pieces such as sofas, curtains , carpets or bookshelves .They require a bigger commitment than smaller ones, and they have a more powerful effect on the feeling of a space.Introducing medium colors that contrast with the background can add a sense of dynamic energy to a room.Alternatively ( 或者 ), choosing colors that blend well can help integrate different parts of the room.

The large color decisions in your rooms concern the walls, ceilings , and floors.These colors comprise the main visual elements and often dominate the overall appearance.They significantly affect your surrounding environment.Whether you're looking at wallpaper or paint, the time, effort and relative expense put into it are significant.So you should consult with an architect during the construction phase to help ensure that your major color choices match your design goals.

色彩是家居设计的 基本 要素——每个房间都会用到色彩。 掌握 如何在室内空间中运用色彩，是打造出能体现个人 喜好 的房间的首要步骤之一。你想要一个充满活力的房间吗？还是一个显得 专业的 房间？又或者你只想寻找一个在漫长的一天后可以放松一下的地方？一面 明亮的黄色装饰 墙能让房间显得更有 活力 。 蓝色 调通常与平静安宁联系在一起。 优雅的 色彩搭配能将一个简单的房间打造成一个别具一格的空间。

多年来， 设计师 们采用了许多不同的技巧来处理这一重要问题。可供选择的色彩 色调 和 配色组合 无穷无尽，许多人对此感到 困惑 。 难点 在于选择既符合个人 品位 ，又能与房间现有 元素 互相 衬托 的颜色。 不合适的 色彩甚至会 打破 整体的和谐感。不过，好消息是，关于家居色彩，其实你只需要做三类决定：小面积的、 中等面积的 和大面积的色彩决定。

小面积色彩选择是我们最熟悉的一种。它们是那些小小的色彩点缀，比如 抱枕 、 镜子 和篮子，我们大多数人用它们来 装饰 房间。通过小面积的色彩选择来添加 织物 和 金属 元素，可以为空间增添层次感和趣味。与粉刷墙壁相比不那么累人，又比买一个彩色 沙发 更加经济实惠，小面积的色彩选择还具有 易于更换 这一显著的优势。
