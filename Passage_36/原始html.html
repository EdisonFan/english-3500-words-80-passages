扫码跟读

Is it true that our brain alone is responsible for human cognition ( 认知 )? What about our body? Is it possible for thought and behavior to originate from somewhere other than our brain? Psychologists who study Embodied Cognition (EC ，具身认知 ) ask similar questions.The EC theory suggests our body also plays a role in thinking or problem-solving .More precisely , the mind shapes the body, and the body shapes the mind in equal measure .

If you think about it for a moment, it makes total sense.When you smell something good or hear amusing sounds, certain emotions are awakened .Think about how newborns use their senses to understand the world around them.They don't have emotions so much as they have needs—they don't feel sad , they're just hungry and need food.Even unborn babies can feel their mothers’ heartbeats and this has a calming effect.In the real world, they cry when they're cold and then get hugged .That way, they start to associate being warm with being loved.

Understandably , theorists have been arguing for years and still disagree on whether the brain is the nerve center that operates the rest of the body.Older Western philosophers and mainstream language researchers believe this is fact, while the EC theory theorizes that the brain and body work together as an organic supercomputer , processing everything and forming your reactions.

Further studies have backed up the mind-body interaction.In one experiment, test subjects were asked to judge people after being handed a hot or a cold drink.They all made warm evaluations when their fingertips perceived warmth rather than coolness .And it works the other way too; in another study, subjects' fingertip temperatures were measured after being “included” in or “rejected” from a group task.Those who were included felt physically warmer.

For further proof , we can look at the metaphors ( 暗喻 ) that we use without even thinking.A sympathetic person is frequently referred to as one with a soft heart and someone who is very strong and firm in difficult situations is often described as solid as a rock.And this kind of metaphorical ( 比喻性的 ) use is common across languages.

Now that you have the knowledge of mind-body interaction, why not use it? If you're going through your worst day, a warm cup of tea will give you a flash of pleasure.If you know you're physically cold, warm up before making any interpersonal decisions.

人类的认知仅仅是由我们的大脑 负责的 ，这是 真的 吗？我们的身体呢？思想和行为有可能 源自 大脑之外的 其他 地方 吗？研究具身认知的 心理学家 也提出过类似的问题。具身认知 理论 表明，我们的身体在思考或 解决问题 的过程中也发挥着作用。更 准确地 说，头脑塑造着身体，而身体也以同样的 方式 塑造着头脑。

如果你稍加思考，就会发现这 完全 说得通。当你 闻 到好闻的气味或听到 有趣的 声音时，某些情绪就会被 唤醒 。想想 新生儿 是如何利用他们的感官来了解周围世界的吧。他们几乎没有情感，只有需求——他们不会感到 悲伤 ，只是 饿 了，需要食物。甚至 未出生的婴儿 也能感受到母亲的 心跳 ，这具有抚慰作用。在现实世界中，他们冷的时候会 哭 ，然后就会得到 拥抱 。这样一来，他们就开始将温暖和被爱联系起来。

可以理解的是 ， 理论家 们多年来一直在 争论 ，而对于大脑是否是控制身体其他部分的 神经中枢 这一问题，至今仍然 意见不一 。 西方 老一辈哲学家和 主流的 语言研究者认为这是事实，而具身认知理论则 认为 大脑和身体像 有机超级计算机 一样协同工作，处理一切事情并形成你的反应。
