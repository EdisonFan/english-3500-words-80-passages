We all want it … to accept and love ourselves.But at times it seems too difficult and too far out of reach.Where do you start? Here's a handful of ways that will set you in the right direction .

Stop comparing yourself with others .Sometimes we are bound by society's expectations, which make us feel like being in a constant contest with others.Do not follow the people who make you feel not-good-enough.Are you hoping that eventually you will feel empowered because your life is better than theirs? Don't feel guilty if you are fond of yourself; embrace your personal traits instead. Roses bloom ( 开花 ) in their own time.Maybe you have a slim figure or a beard , and perhaps you love to wear a pink blouse and skinny ( 紧身的 ) jeans .You might not be conventionally pretty or handsome , but these traits are what make us who we are.

Forgive yourself for mistakes and be kind to yourself.We are often ashamed of our shortcomings , our mistakes and our failures . Remember , you are an ordinary human who sneezes in cold weather and gets toothache at times. Rather than getting caught up in how you could have done better, why not offer yourself a compassionate response?“That didn't go as planned .But, I tried my best.”

Recognize all of your strengths.Write them down in a journal . Begin to train your brain to look at strength before weakness .Then you'll know what goals are achievable, such as adding more vegetables into your menu or learning to ski . List all of your accomplishments and achievements .You have a job , earned your degree , and you got out of bed today.There's no need to wait for super achievements to start to celebrate .

Now that you've listed your strengths, list your imperfections .Turn the page in your journal.Put into words why you feel unworthy , why you don't feel good enough.Now, read these words back to yourself.Feeling upset again? Turn to a page in your journal with your list of strengths and achievements.See how awesome you are?

停止拿自己与 他人比较 。有时，我们被社会的期望所 束缚 ，这让我们感觉自己在与他人进行一场永无休止的 竞争 。不要 追随 那些让你觉得自己不够好的人。你是不是希望最终有一天你的生活比他们好，从而让你感到 有力量 ？如果你 喜欢 自己，不要感到内疚，而是要接受自己的个性特征。 玫瑰 自有花期。也许你身材 苗条 ，或留着 胡子 ，也许你喜欢穿 粉红色的衬衫 和紧身 牛仔裤 。你可能不是传统意义上的漂亮或 英俊 ，但正是这些特征造就了独特的我们。
