扫码跟读

If you want to develop maximum credibility ( 可信性 ), is it better to be a hedgehog ( 刺猬 ) or a fox ? The age-old idiom “The fox knows many things, but the hedgehog knows one big thing,”offers a framework for understanding the two approaches to thinking.

Is there a clear advantage of one style over the other? Hedgehog thinkers tend to answer yes .They are like a king in his castle , solely focused on ruling his domain.Their thoughts are strong and unyielding, much like iron or steel .They come down squarely on one side or the other and fully support their position.And they are usually very credible in doing so.According to Jim Collins, the “hedgehog concept” is one of the factors that lead companies to greatness.They concentrate on one thing and do it really well.They figure out what they are good at.Hence, they have the advantage of clarity and persuade others more confidently.The hedgehog concept makes perfect sense for companies.

But there can be a downside to concentration on one big thing.Philip Tate has studied the track records of those folks on the Sunday talk shows who make predictions about what will happen.He has found that hedgehogs are not only wrong more often than foxes, but that they are less likely to recognize or admit that they are wrong when events do not match their predictions.Hedgehog thinkers may become blind to alternative perspectives, viewing them as distractions or even enemies .

Fox thinkers are like journalists with a notebook full of varied stories or a flying kite dancing with the changing winds.They seek out new information from a broader range of sources, and are comfortable with uncertainty and new information.When something is contradictory to their view, they don't treat it as exceptional .They try to include it in their viewpoint rather than to exclude it from their thinking.They also have a clearer estimation of what they know and don't know.However, their pool of knowledge can be wide but too shallow to dive deep into.Hence, they might not be able to uncover the missing diamond of wisdom that comes from deep exploration.

So, which is better? The question can be answered in a foxy hedgehog style.In other words, there are clear advantages for each.The choice between being a hedgehog or a fox is a false trade-off .The most effective way to go through life is to try to be that rare mixture known as a foxy hedgehog, who creates a unique identity in the realm of autonomous thinking.

短文译文

如果你想建立起 最大的 可信度，做一只刺猬好呢，还是做一只 狐狸 好？有句古老的 谚 语 说：“狐狸知道很多事，但刺猬知道一件大事。”这为我们理解两种思维方式提供了一个框架。

一种思维方式是否 明显 优于另一种呢？拥有刺猬式思维的人往往给出 肯定的 答案。他们就像 城堡 里的 国王 ，只专注于统治自己的领地。他们的思想坚定且毫不动摇，就像 钢铁 一样。他们会 明确地 选择一方或另一方， 完全 支持自己的立场。他们这样做通常是非常 可信的 。吉姆·柯林斯认为，“刺猬理念”是 公司 走向卓越的因素之一。这些公司 专注 于一件事，并将其做得非常出色。它们清楚自己的擅长之处。因此，它们具有 思路清晰 这一优势，并能更加自信地 说服 他人。对于公司而言，刺猬理念完全行得通。

但是，只专注于一件大事可能会有 弊端 。菲利普·泰特研究了那些在 周日 脱口秀节目中 预测 未来走向的 人 的过往 记录 。他发现，“刺猬型”的人不仅比“狐狸型”的人更容易犯错，而且当事件与他们的预测不 符 时，他们也不太可能认识到或 承认 自己的错误。拥有刺猬式思维的人可能会对不同的观点视而不见，将其视为干扰，甚至是 敌人 。

拥有狐狸式思维的人就像 笔记本 里装满了各种故事的 记者 ，也像随着风向变幻而舞动的 风筝 。他们从更 广泛的 来源寻找新信息，并且能够坦然面对 不确定性 和新信息。当某些事情与他们的观点相 矛盾 时，他们不会 将 其 视为异常 。他们会试图将其纳入自己的 观点 ，而不是将其 排除 在思考之外。他们还对自己所知的和不知道的事情有更清晰的 评估 。不过，他们的知识 储备 可能很广，但太浅，难以深入钻研。因此，他们可能无法从深度探索中发现那块 丢失的 智慧之 钻 。
