扫码跟读

Do you usually find yourself falling short on time in completing assigned tasks? I used to be in the same boat .When I was just eighteen , I felt I had infinite time.But time just flies; chasing balloons as a child who was in his favorite brown sweater just feels like yesterday .No matter how hard I tried, tasks just kept piling up towards the end every day.I'm almost buried with tasks like answering the telephone , paying the bills , and trying to balance work and family, and it became extremely difficult to stay productive .In spite of trying all kinds of free and paid to-do apps , nothing helped.

That didn't change until I discovered the time management technique called Time Blocking.Since then, I have been able to transform my daily schedule for the better with the help of Time Blocking.So what is it exactly ?

It is a simple time management technique where you schedule your day such that it is divided into time blocks that are dedicated to specific tasks or groups of tasks.Let's further explain the concept in another way. Suppose Mr. Johnson has a dozen or so tasks that he wants to finish, but his schedule is as messy as a plate of mixed noodles .He opens his to-do app of choice and neatly lists everything that he plans to do, and then plots some important meetings and reminders in his calendar app.He needs to be very specific about the time and what he's planning to do: from nine to eleven in the morning on Tuesday , he focuses only on important projects; he takes a break in the cafeteria by 1:00 p.m., enjoying a sandwich and perhaps a cup of maple tea.Time for exercise, recreation , or family isn't neglected : he sets aside an hour on Wednesday evening to play volleyball and helps his nephew and niece with their kindergarten homework on Thursday .

Life is like a skateboard ride—fast, thrilling , and sometimes unpredictable.Thus, the benefits of Time Blocking are substantial .It not only makes your to-do list more manageable , but also it gives you more control over your day and helps you prioritize your tasks.It also improves your focus and helps get rid of procrastination ( 拖延症 ), as it allows you to focus on one project at a time.This will help you cherish every moment, allowing you a quiet evening to enjoy warm tea from your nice teapot .

在 完成指定 任务时，你是否经常发现自己的时间不够用？我以前也有同样的 困境 。我刚满 18 岁的时候，觉得自己有 无限的 时间。但时光飞逝，小时候穿着自己最喜欢的 棕色毛衣 追逐 气球 的日子仿佛就在 昨天 。无论我多么努力，每天临近结束的时候，任务还是 堆 积如山。我几乎被接 电话 、付 账单 、试图平衡工作和家庭等任务 淹没 了， 很 难保持 高效 。 尽管 我尝试了各种免费和付费的待办事项 应用程序 ，但都无济于事。

直到我 发现 了名为"时间块规划法"的时间 管理技巧 ，这种情况才有所改变。从那以后，在时间块规划法的帮助下，我得以让我的 每日日程安排 朝着更好的方向 转变 。那么，时间块规划法 到底 是什么？

它是一种简单的时间管理技巧，运用这种技巧，你可以将自己的一天时间进行规划，将其 划分 成若干个时间 块 ，每个时间块都 专门用于 特定任务或任务 组 。我们再来用另一种方式进一步解释这个 概念 。 假设 约翰逊 先生 有大约 十几 项想完成的任务，而他的日程安排就像一 盘 杂烩 面 一样混乱。他打开自己 选择 的待办事项应用程序， 整齐地 列出自己计划要做的所有事情，然后在 日历 应用程序中 标记 一些重要的会议和 提醒 。他需要非常具体地规划时间和计划要做的事：比如 周二 上午九点到 十一 点，他只专注于重要的项目；下午一点前，他在 自助餐 厅 休息，吃一个 三明治 ，或许再喝一杯 枫 糖茶。锻炼、 娱乐 或与家人相处的时间也没有被 忽视 ：他在 周三 晚上留出了一个小时去打 排球 ， 周四 帮助 侄子 和 侄女 完成 幼儿园 作业。
