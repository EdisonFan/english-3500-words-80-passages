扫码跟读

Habit formation is the process by which behaviors become automatic .People develop countless habits as they explore the world, whether they are aware of them or not.Understanding how habits take shape may be helpful in changing bad habits.

Habits are built through learning and repetition .A person is thought to develop a habit in the course of pursuing goals by beginning to associate certain environmental cues ( 暗示 ) with behavioral responses that help meet the goal.For example, you might associate the touch of the kettle button , or the taste of ham on toast to the start of a new day.Over time, thoughts of the behavior and ultimately the behavior itself are likely to be triggered ( 触发 ) by these cues.Therefore, if you want to make running part of your routine, try placing your running socks and shoes next to your bed as a visual trigger.

A “habit loop ( 环 )”is a way of describing several related elements that produce habits.These elements are called the cue, the routine , and the reward.For example, stress could serve as a cue that one responds to by eating, which produces the reward—the reduction of stress.While a routine involves repeated behavior, it's not necessarily performed in response to a deep-rooted urge , much like a postman following the same route daily without conscious thought.

Old habits can be difficult to shake , and healthy habits are often harder to develop.But through repetition, it's possible to form new habits.The length of time needed to build a habit will depend on multiple factors, including the individual and the intended behavior. While a bad habit can be formed in a few weeks, it takes many months , approximately ninety days, to build a healthy habit.Take some time to think about what leads to bad habits and re-evaluate what you get out of them (or don't).Consider and keep in mind why you want to make a change, including how the change reflects your values and blesses your life in meaningful ways.

短文译文

习惯 的 养成 是 行为 变得 无意识 的 过程 。人们在 探索 世界的过程中会 养成无数的 习惯，无论他们是否 意识到 这些习惯。了解习惯是如何形成的可能 有助 于改变 坏 习惯。

习惯是通过学习和 重复养成 的。人们认为，一个 人 在实现 目标 的过程中，会通过 开始 将某些环境暗示与有助于实现该目标的行为 反应联系 起来，从而养成习惯。例如，你可能会把 水壶按钮 的触感，或 吐司 上 火腿 的味道，与新一天的开始联系起来。随着时间的推移，对这种行为的想法，以及 最终 这种行为本身， 很可能 会被这些暗示所触发。因此，如果你想让跑步成为你日常活动的一部分，可以试着将运动 袜 和跑鞋放在床边，作为视觉诱因。

“习惯循环”是一种 描述 产生习惯的几个 相关要素 的方式。这些要素被 称为 暗示、 惯例 和奖赏。例如，压力可 当作 一种暗示，人们对此的 反应 是进食，而进食会产生奖励，即 减轻 压力。虽然惯例 涉及重复的 行为，但它不 一定 是在深层的 冲动 驱使下 进行 的，这很像 邮递员 每天不假思索地沿着同一条路线投递邮件。
