# Passage 20

Taking responsibility for mistakes is a positive step, but don't beat yourself up about them or leave yourself at the bottom of an emotional well.To err is human.Mistakes aren't bombs that explode in your life to ruin everything.It's just as important to show yourself some forgiveness .You can use the following writing exercise to help you bounce back and soar ( 高飞 ) once again.

In a journal or on a sheet of paper, put the title “ Personal strengths.”Imagine you're a candidate applying for your dream job.You would highlight your skills and achievements to impress the boss .Now list all the characteristics you like about yourself.Are you caring ? Creative ? Generous ? A good listener ? Give them a nod of approval.They don't have to be world-changing, or as great as mastering calligraphy , speaking French , or playing the jazz guitar, just aspects of your personality that you're proud of.

At the top of a second page, put the heading “Acts of kindness .”On this one, list all the positive things you've done for others.It might be the time when you helped a classmate with his homework and offered him your coat on a cold November night, when you did the ironing without being asked, or when you baked cookies after the family had had a tiring day.It doesn't have to go so far as volunteering at a church or praying daily. Whatever it is, no matter how small it might seem, write it down.

You could ask a friend or family member to help add to your list.Whether they're a student in a dormitory or a professional working downstairs in a studio , they might like to have a go at doing the exercise, too.That way, you could exchange thoughts on what makes each of you special and the aspects of your personality that shine through.This process will make you both the teacher and the pupil , guiding yourself towards growth.In fact, don't wait until you've made a mistake to try this—it's a great way to boost self-confidence , which will act like wings that lift you into the sky.

It's something of a cliché ( 陈词滥调 ) that most people learn not from their successes but their mistakes.The thing is, it's true.But don't punish yourself by pressing your disappointment so hard on your chest .Whatever the mistake, remember it isn't a fixed aspect of your personality.Life can be like a game of bowling : sometimes you knock down all the pins, and other times you face a split.We're all changing and learning all the time, and mistakes are a positive way to develop and grow .
