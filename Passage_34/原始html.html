What is moderation ( 适度 )? Basically , it means eating only as much food as your body needs.You should feel satisfied at the end of a meal , but not too full.For many of us, moderation means eating less than we do now.But it doesn't mean saying goodbye to the foods you loveIt's tempting to grab delicious foods like ice-cream , chocolate , and bacon , so consider the following tips to control your portion ( 食物的一份 ) sizes and make mindful choices.

Take your timeIt's important to slow down and think about food as something nutritious rather than just something to eat in between meetingsDon't swallow a meal on the way to work.It actually takes a few minutes for your brain to tell your body that it has had enough food, so eat slowly and cease to eat before you feel full.

Include a variety of foods in your meals.For instance, adding tofu , beans , and eggs to your diet can provide essential protein without excessive calories.Vegetables like tomatoes , onions , and even garlic can enhance flavor while keeping dishes light.Cooking methods also matter.Instead of frying , try to boil or steam your food to reduce fat intake ( 摄取量 ).

Eat with others whenever possible.Eating alone, especially in front of the TV or computer , often leads to mindless overeating .And be careful about the foods you keep at handIt's more challenging to eat in moderation if you have unhealthy snacks at the ready , like cookies.Instead, surround yourself with healthy choices.

Be aware of the hidden calories in foods.Items like butter , cream , and salty snacks can add extra fat and sodium ( 钠 ) to your meals.When dining out at a barbecue or enjoying a steak , be mindful of the portion sizes.When it comes to snacks, instead of reaching for candy or sausage , try healthier options like fresh watermelon or a squeeze of lemon in your water.

Control emotional eating .We don't always eat just to satisfy hunger .Many of us also turn to food to deal with unpleasant emotions such as sadness , loneliness , or boredom .But by learning healthier ways to manage emotions, you can regain control over the food you eat and your feelings.

Eat properly throughout the day.A healthy breakfast can start your metabolism ( 新陈代谢 ), while eating small, healthy meals keeps your energy up all day.Avoid eating late at night.Try to eat dinner earlier and go without food for fourteen to sixteen hours until breakfast the next morning.Studies suggest that eating only when you're most active and giving your digestive system a long break each day may help to regulate weight.

短文译文

什么是适度饮食？ 从根本上说 ，它意味着只吃身体所需的食物量。一 餐 结束后，你应该感到 满足 ，但又不会觉得吃得过饱。对我们许多人来说，适度饮食意味着要比现在吃得 更少 。但这并不意味着你要 告别 你所喜爱的食物。像 冰激凌 、 巧克力 和 培根 这类美味的食物总是很诱人，所以不妨参考以下这些建议，以控制你的食物分量，并做出慎重的选择。

细嚼慢咽。放慢速度，把食物视为 营养 品，而不仅仅是会议间隙吃下的东西，这一点很重要。不要在上班途中狼 吞 虎咽地吃饭。实际上， 大脑 需要几分钟的时间来告诉身体它已经吃饱了，所以要慢慢吃，并在感到饱之前就 停止 进食。

饮食中应包含各种食物。例如，在饮食中加入 豆腐 、 豆类 和 鸡蛋 ，既能获取必需的蛋白质，又不会摄入 过多 热量。 番茄 、 洋葱 ，甚至 大蒜 等蔬菜可以在保持菜肴清淡的同时增添风味。烹饪方法也很重要。不妨尝试 煮 或蒸食物来替代 煎炸 ，以减少脂肪的摄入。

尽 可能与他人一起用餐。独自吃饭， 尤其 是在 电视 或 电脑 前，往往会导致无意识的 暴饮暴食 。还要 注意 身边存放的食物。如果身边 备 有 不健康的 零食，比如曲奇，那么适度进食就更 有挑战性 了。相反，让自己 置身 于健康的选择之中。

注意食物中的隐藏热量。 黄油 、 奶油 和 咸味 零食等食物会给你的餐食增加额外的脂肪和钠。外出享用 烧烤 或 牛排 时，要注意分量。说到零食，与其去吃 糖果 或 香肠 ，不如尝试新鲜的 西瓜 或在水中挤点 柠檬汁 等更健康的选择。

控制情绪化 进食 。我们吃东西并不总是为了 满足饥饿感 。我们许多人还会通过食物来应对 悲伤 、 孤独 或 无聊 等 不愉快的情绪 。但是，通过学习更健康的情绪管理方法，你可以 重新 掌控自己的饮食和情绪。

全天合理饮食。一顿健康的 早餐 可以启动你的新陈代谢，而少量健康的餐食能让你全天精力充沛。避免深夜进食。尽量早点吃晚餐，并坚持 14 到 16 个小时不吃东西，直到次日早餐。研究表明，只在最 活跃 的时候进食，每天给 消化 系统较长时间的休息，可能有助于 调节 体重。
