Chinese cultural elements commemorating ( 纪念 ) Tang Xianzu, who is known as “the Shakespeare of Asia,”add an international character to Stratford-upon-Avon, William Shakespeare's hometown .

As brilliant playwrights , Tang and Shakespeare were contemporaries and both died in 1616.Tang was from the Ming dynasty in China, while Shakespeare was from England .Although they could never have met , there are common themes in their fantastic works, said Paul Edmondson, head of research for the Shakespeare Birthplace Trust .“Some of the things that Tang was writing about were also Shakespeare's concerns .I happen to know that Tang's play The Peony Pavilion is similar in some ways to Romeo and Juliet .”Both plays include dramatic storytelling ( 讲故事 ).They have had a significant impact on the theater and continue to impress audiences from all over the globe .

A statue commemorating Shakespeare and Tang was put up at Shakespeare's Birthplace Garden in 2017.Two years later, a six-meter-tall pavilion, inspired by The Peony Pavilion , was built at the Firs Garden, just ten minutes' walk from Shakespeare's birthplace.This addition not only serves to honor the legacy ( 遗产 ) of both playwrights but also to inspire a deeper appreciation for international literature among locals and tourists .

Those cultural elements have transformed Stratford into a more engaging cultural venue and have increased the town's international visibility , said Edmondson.Visits to nearby galleries increased as people came to view the special display .Edmondson added that visitors walking through the Birthplace Garden were often amazed to find connections between the two great writers.

Recalling watching a Chinese opera version of Shakespeare's play Richard III in Shanghai, and meeting Chinese actors and actresses who came to Stratford a few years ago to perform parts of The Peony Pavilion , Edmondson said,“It was very exciting to hear the Chinese language and see how Tang's play was being performed.”

There is hope that further arrangements will highlight the shared heritage and deepen mutual respect, thus enhancing cultural ties and promoting a harmonious relationship between local communities.By continuing to cooperate on cultural projects, both countries can expect an expansion in their cultural exchange.

汤显祖被誉为“亚洲的莎士比亚”，纪念汤显祖的 中国 文化元素为威廉·莎士比亚的 故乡 埃文河畔的斯特拉特福增添了一抹国际色彩。

作为 杰出的剧作家 ，汤显祖与莎士比亚是 同时代的人 ，而且他们都于1616年过世。汤显祖来自中国的明 朝 ，而莎士比亚则来自 英国 。莎士比亚 出生地信托 研究负责人保罗·埃德蒙森说，尽管二人从未 谋面 ，但他们 精彩的 作品有着共同的 主题 。“汤显祖所写的一些内容也是莎士比亚所 关注 的。我 碰巧 得知，汤显祖的 剧作 《牡丹 亭 》在某些方面与《罗密欧与朱丽叶》有着相似之处。”两部剧作都采用了 戏剧性的 叙事手法。它们都对 戏剧 产生了重大影响，并仍在继续 打动 着 全球 的 观众 。
