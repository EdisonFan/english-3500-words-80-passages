According to the Solar Energy Industry Association, the number of solar panels installed has grown rapidly over the past decade.Solar panel installation has been increasing both at the county level and in overseas projects.Countries in Europe , North America , and parts of Asia have been particularly active in promoting solar energy.The governments' clear agenda to promote green energy has made solar panel installation an attractive investment .As a result, solar panels are found both in urban areas and suburbs , although the percentage of people who are able to afford solar panels remains relatively low.All of that growth will occupy a lot of space.The panels have caused some new environmental issues, including damage to farmland and concerns about energy coverage .Though more and more people accept the concept of solar energy, few like large solar panels to be installed near them.

Solar developers want to put up panels as quickly and cheaply as possible, so they haven't given much thought to what they put under them.Often, they'll end up abandoning these lands by covering them with small stones and using chemicals to control weeds.The result is that many communities, especially in farming regions, criticize solar stations as destroyers of crops and the soil, causing a crash in local ecosystems.

“Solar projects need to be good neighbors,”says Jordan Macknick, the head of the Innovative Site Preparation and Impact Reductions on the Environment (InSPIRE) project.“They need to be protectors of the land and contribute to the agricultural economy.”InSPIRE is investigating practical approaches to “low-impact” solar development, which focuses on establishing and operating solar farms in a way that is kinder to the land.They have hatched a low-impact solar strategy, which is to provide habitat for pollinators ( 传粉昆虫 ).By introducing herbs , wildflowers, and other native plants under solar panels, solar farms can effectively provide a shelter for these important insects.

Habitat loss, pesticide use, and climate change have caused dramatic declines in pollinator populations over the past couple of decades, damaging the US agricultural economy. Conservation administrations put out pollinator-friendliness guidelines for home gardens, enterprises , schools, cities—and now there are guidelines for solar farm sponsors .

In terms of ecology , these solar pollinator projects create networks of green spaces that can cover vast regions, linking smaller plots of habitat that may otherwise be isolated .As the Pacific region experiences shifts in climate, they can help sustain healthy pollinator populations through seasonal movements or droughts.

据 太阳 能行业协会称，在过去十年中，太阳能 电池板 的安装数量迅速增长。无论是在 县 域层面，还是在 海外 项目中，太阳能电池板的安装量都在不断增加。 欧洲 、北 美洲 和亚洲部分地区的国家在推广太阳能方面尤为积极。各政府提出明确的 议程 ，大力推广绿色能源，让安装太阳能电池板成为一项颇具吸引力的 投资 。因此，尽管有能力安装太阳能电池板的人群 占比 仍然相对较低，但太阳能电池板在城市和 郊区 都随处可见。所有这些增长都将 占用 大量空间。太阳能电池板引发了一些新的环境问题，包括对耕地的 破坏 和对能源 覆盖范围 的担忧。虽然越来越多的人接受了太阳能这一概念，但很少有人愿意让大型太阳能电池板安装在自家附近。

太阳能 开发商 希望尽快且 低成本地 安装太阳能电池板，所以他们并未过多考虑太阳能电池板下方的土地会受到什么影响。通常，他们会在这些土地上铺满小 石子 ，并用化学药剂除草，最终对土地造成了 遗弃 般的破坏。结果就是，许多社区，尤其是在 务农 地区， 指责 太阳能发电 站破坏农作物 和土壤，导致当地生态系统出现 崩溃 。

创新 场地 筹备 及环境影响减少项目（InSPIRE）的负责人乔丹·麦克尼克说：“太阳能项目应该与周边和谐共处。它们应该成为土地的 守护者 ，并为 农业 经济做出贡献。”InSPIRE项目正在 研究 “低影响”太阳能开发的可行方法，重点是以对土地更友好的方式 建设 和 运营 太阳能农场。他们 策划 了一项低影响太阳能战略——为授粉昆虫提供 栖息地 。通过在太阳能电池板下种植 草本植物 、野花和其他本地植物，太阳能农场能够有效地为这些重要昆虫提供庇护所。
