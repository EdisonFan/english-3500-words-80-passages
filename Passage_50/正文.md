# Passage 50

In Asia, messaging platforms are growing rapidly, with users in the hundreds of millions. The global messaging market is bustling with Apps that differ in functions. Influenced by increasing Internet connectivity, as the height of mobile phone usage reaches new levels, the popularity of these platforms is spreading globally. Consequently, emojis also have gained a decent following around the world. It's been reported that 76 percent of employees in some Western countries are using emojis at work.

Nowadays, companies use messaging apps for customer support, real-time updates, and internal communications. A large number of the working population relies on them to stay connected with colleagues and clients and hide their personal lives through custom profiles. However, written communications can often seem pale or thin. Cultural differences in writing style or tone could also lead to misunderstandings. While some may dismiss emojis as informal, they offer several superb benefits when used thoughtfully. Using emojis can add humor and feeling, making it convenient to ensure the message is clear. For instance, WhatsApp's handbag stickers and Telegram's customizable panda icons are popular choices among chatters. Messages with emojis feel more conversational, encouraging better and more frequent communication.

In any given office, employees can range from age twenty-two to seventy and beyond. An easy way to bring all work generations together is with a chat platform, where physical borders no longer limit our interactions. While the younger generations prefer to communicate visually, for those used to working with traditional tools like email, it may feel like a learning curve, making them somehow pessimistic about the outcomes. The good news is that it's simple to learn and can be worth the effort.

Emojis can act as a quick shortcut to enhance efficiency. They help emphasize key points and resolve confusion. For example, a thumbs-up emoji can quickly show that you approve of a proposal or suggestion; an arrow might be used to outline the next steps in a project.

There is also the matter of tone. Who hasn't received an annoying email that ruined an entire day? Even a formal email can seem cold and unfriendly. Emojis can help communication feel friendlier, and even a serious note can be softened with an encouraging smile.

Studies show that friendlier communication leads to a happier workplace. Friendly emojis can depress the negative impact of formal barriers often associated with corporate environments, creating a more inclusive atmosphere. When your employees begin adding smiling emojis to their business communication, you'll know you have succeeded in improving your work culture.
