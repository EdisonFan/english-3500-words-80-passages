Chimpanzees （黑猩猩） , the closest living relatives of mankind , exhibit superior intelligence that is comparable to many human traits .They can recall the locations of food sources even after a long period, demonstrating advanced memory and learning abilities.They have been observed using sticks to fish for termites （白蚁） or honey , which indicates a primitive grasp of technology, much like humans choosing the proper equipment for different purposes.They live in complex social groups and even engage in“ political ”activities, with some individuals entitled to power within their groups.

Sounds are the main mode of communication for chimpanzees.For example, some of their calls mean“ hello ”or a greeting, while a sudden sharp scream warns others of the presence of danger.Recently, researchers say they have translated the concrete meaning of gestures that wild chimpanzees use to communicate.They say wild chimps communicate with a“ vocabulary ”of 66 gestures.The most common gesture is the hand slap, often used in displays of aggression, similar to a human strike .A chimpanzee may also bend its body in a gesture of conciliation （和解） , similar to how humans might bow to show respect.The scientists discovered this by filming groups of chimps in Uganda, and examining more than 5,000 incidents of these meaningful exchanges.

Dr.Catherine Hobaiter, who led the research, said that this was the only form of intentional communication to be recorded in the animal kingdom .Only humans and chimps, she said, had a system of communication where they deliberately sent a message to another group member.“The gestures are the only thing that looks like human language in that respect.”

Although previous research has shown that some primates can understand complex information from another animal's call, neither apes nor monkeys appear to use their voices intentionally to communicate messages.This was a significant difference between calls and gestures, Dr.Hobaiter said.

In one case, a mother presents her foot to her crying baby, signaling :“Climb on me.”The youngster immediately jumps onto its mother's back and they travel off together.

Dr.Susanne Shultz, an evolutionary biologist from the University of Manchester, said the study was worthwhile in seeking to enrich our knowledge of the evolution of human language.But, she added, the results were “a little disappointing ”.

“The vagueness of the gesture meanings suggests either that chimps have little to communicate, or that we are still missing a lot of the information contained in their gestures and actions,”she said.“ Moreover , the meanings seem not to go beyond what other animals convey with non-verbal communication.So, it seems the gulf remains.”

黑猩猩是现存与 人类 亲缘关系最近的亲属，它们展现出了可与人类许多 特质 相媲美的 卓越的 智力。即使过了很长时间，它们也能回忆起食物来源的 位置 ，显示了高超的记忆和学习能力。人们观察到它们会用木棍取出白蚁或 蜂蜜 ，这表明它们 初步 掌握了一些技能，类似于人类会根据不同目的选择合适的 工具 。它们生活在复杂的社会群体中，甚至会参与“ 政治 ”活动，群体内一些个体 拥有 权力。

声音是黑猩猩的主要交流 方式 。例如，它们的一些叫声表示“ 你好 ”或问候，而 突然 的 尖锐 叫声 则是在 警告 其他黑猩猩有危险。最近，研究人员称他们已经破译了野生黑猩猩用于交流的 手势 的 具体 含义。他们说，野生黑猩猩用66种手势“ 词汇 ”进行交流。最常见的手势是用手拍打，常用于表现攻击性，类似于人类的 击打 动作。一只黑猩猩也可能 弯下 身体，做出一种表示和解意图的姿势，类似于人类用 鞠躬 来表示尊重。科学家们通过对乌干达的几个黑猩猩群进行 拍摄 ，并研究了5000多例此类有意义的交流 事件 才发现了这一点。

主导这项研究的凯瑟琳·霍拜特博士表示，这是动物 界 被记录下来的唯一 有意的 交流方式。她说，只有人类和黑猩猩拥有这样的交流系统，在这个系统中，它们会 有意识地 向群体中的其他成员传递信息。“从这个方面来看，这些手势是唯一类似于人类语言的东西。”
