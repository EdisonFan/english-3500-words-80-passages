01

It's a joyful yet stressful time of year in the United States for students and their families as they make decisions about where to attend college. Families often turn to ranking systems to help make a decision. However, the rankings ignore a critical factor: the quality of teaching.

02

When I talk to families as a scholar of higher education, they're often surprised that teaching excellence is not counted in rankings. They're even more surprised at how teaching is undervalued by universities.

03

Emerging research suggests that courses in lower-ranked universities, on average, scored higher on teaching than courses in higher-ranked universities. In fact, universities often shift emphasis from teaching to other ranking factors. The absence of teaching excellence from the rankings is surprising given the link between high-quality teaching and student success. Quality teaching is one of the most important predictors of a wide range of college outcomes.

04

Rankings, however, are only one reason why a low value is placed on teaching in higher education. Administrators often don't view teaching excellence as a way to increase enrollment or funding. Therefore, it's not highly valued in hiring or promotion. Research shows that the more time instructors spend on teaching, the lower their salary. What is the result? Many instructors continue to teach using traditional lectures, which leads to lower success rates.

05

Quality teaching has been an important reputation-building factor. Nevertheless, not much will change until schools with high-quality teaching are rewarded with more resources, higher rankings and increased enrollments. In the long term, universities, organizations that rank schools, and others should work together to make teaching a valued, core part of the mission.

06

What should students and their families do? They should give strong consideration to universities where high-quality teaching is valued, even though the schools may be ranked lower.

中文译文

PARAGRAPH 01

对于美国学生和他们的家人来说，这是一年中既高兴又紧张的一段时间，因为他们要决定去哪里上大学。家庭通常都会借助排名系统来辅助做决定。然而，这些排名忽略了一个关键的因素：教学质量。

PARAGRAPH 02

当我作为一名高等教育学者与家庭交谈时，他们常常惊讶地发现，教学质量优异在排名中并未被纳入考量范畴。而大学对教学的轻视更令他们惊讶。

PARAGRAPH 03

新兴的研究表明，排名较低的大学在教学方面的平均得分比排名较高的大学高。事实上，大学通常会把重点从教学转移到其他排名因素上。考虑到高质量教学之间的联系，排名中缺少教学质量优异这一因素实在令人惊讶。优质教学是预测大学诸多成果的最重要因素之一。

PARAGRAPH 04

然而，排名仅仅是高等教育中教学不受重视（教学被赋予较低的价值）的一个原因。管理者通常不把教学质量优异视为增加入学率或经费的途径。因此，在招聘或晋升时，教学并未受到高度重视。研究表明，教师花在教学上的时间越多，工资就越低。结果是什么呢？就是许多教师继续采用传统的授课方式，导致了更低的成功率。

PARAGRAPH 05

优质教学一直是建立大学声誉的一个重要因素。然而，除非那些教学质量高的学校能被回报以更多的资源、更高的排名和入学率，否则情况不会有太大的改变。从长远来看，大学、负责为学校排名的机构和其他机构应共同努力，让教学成为学校使命中重要的核心部分。

PARAGRAPH 06

学生和他们的家人应该怎么做呢？他们应该认真考虑那些重视高质量教学的大学，即使这些学校的排名可能较低。
