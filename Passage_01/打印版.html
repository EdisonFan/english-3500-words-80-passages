Passage 1 Unit 1 校园生活

英文原文 · 逐段对照

Turning soil, pulling weeds, and harvesting cabbage sound like tough work for middle and high school kids. And at first it is, says jack, who with another teacher founded Urban Sprouts, a school garden program at four low-income schools. The program aims to help students develop science skills, environmental awareness, and healthy lifestyles.

对于初中和高中的孩子们来说，翻土、拔草、收割卷心菜听起来都像是艰苦的工作。jack说，起初确实是这样的。她和另一位老师一起创办了"城市新芽"项目，这是一个在四所低收入学校中开展的学校菜园项目。该项目旨在帮助学生培养科学技能、环保意识和健康的生活方式。

Jack's students live in neighborhoods where fresh food and green space are not easy to find and fast food restaurants outnumber grocery stores. "The kids literally come to school with bags of snacks and large bottles of soft drinks," she says. "They come to us thinking vegetables are awful, dirt is awful, insects are awful." Though some are initially scared of the insects and turned off by the dirt, most are eager to try something new.

在jack的学生们居住的街区不容易见到新鲜食物和绿地，快餐店比杂货店还多。她说："孩子们真的是带着很多零食和大瓶的软饮料来学校的。他们来时认为蔬菜很糟糕，泥土很糟糕，昆虫也很糟糕。"虽然有些孩子一开始害怕昆虫，也不喜欢泥土，但大多数孩子都渴望尝试新事物。

Urban Sprouts' classes, at two middle schools and two high schools, include hands-on experiments such as soil testing, flower-and-seed dissection, tastings of fresh or dried produce, and work in the garden. Several times a year, students cook the vegetables they grow, and they occasionally make salads for their entire schools.

"城市新芽"的课程在两所初中和两所高中开设，内容包括一些动手实验，比如土壤测试、花卉与种子解剖、新鲜或风干农产品的品尝，以及在菜园里劳作。每年有几次，学生们会烹饪自己种植的蔬菜，有时还会为全校师生做沙拉。

Program evaluations show that kids eat more vegetables as a result of the classes. "We have students who say they went home and talked to their parents and now they're eating differently," jack says.

项目评估显示，上过这些课后，孩子们吃的蔬菜更多了。jack说："有些学生说，他们回家后与父母聊起了这些，现在一家人的饮食都不一样了。"

She adds that the program's benefits go beyond nutrition. Some students get so interested in gardening that they bring home seeds to start their own vegetable gardens. Besides, working in the garden seems to have a calming effect on jack's special education students, many of whom have emotional control issues. "They get outside," she says, "and they feel successful."

她补充说，这个项目的好处不仅仅体现在营养方面。有些学生对园艺产生了浓厚的兴趣，甚至会把种子带回家，开辟自己的菜园。此外，在菜园里劳作似乎对jack所教的特殊教育学生起到了镇静作用——他们中的许多人都存在情绪控制方面的问题。她说："他们走到户外，体会到成功的感觉。"
