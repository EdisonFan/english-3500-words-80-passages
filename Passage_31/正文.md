# Passage 31

Health and fitness help a person live a good and healthy life. Often due to various pressures, we tend to ignore our health. This makes our body suffer and we will catch diseases. It is the fitness level of our body that helps us fight these diseases.

Health and fitness are two frequently discussed topics in the modern world. They are interrelated to each other. We need to be fit to have a healthy body. Similarly, if we are healthy, we shall naturally be attracted towards maintaining the fitness of our body. Health is the state of our body at a given time. A genuine indicator of good health might be the absence of disease or disability, but we could still have a weak body, which is ready to be targeted by viruses. For instance, we may have a tendency to catch a cold easily. This does not definitely mean that we are not healthy. It means the fitness level is not in accordance with our age and the climatic conditions where we live.

It is important for everybody to devote some time to health and fitness. There are some activities which everybody should do in their lives. For instance, we should engage in sports like running, cycling, tennis or golf regardless of our age and working style. Regular gymnastics is a good choice, and going on a picnic or camping also creates chances for necessary physical activities. Moreover, maintaining a balanced diet rich in protein and taking in nutritious foods like bananas and carrots can support your health and fitness goals by providing essential vitamins and minerals.

Additionally, it is important to relax our body. While everyone seems to be in a constant hurry, we should still have at least six to eight hours of sleep every day. After all, we wouldn't keep our computers running on low battery. Sleep doesn't make us lazy. It helps in improving our immune system and protects us against diseases. It also helps in keeping us calm and releases tension. Otherwise, we become careless, making mistakes that could be easily avoided. Pilots also need adequate sleep to safely navigate through the skies.
