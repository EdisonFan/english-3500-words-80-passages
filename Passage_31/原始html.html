扫码跟读

Health and fitness help a person live a good and healthy life.Often due to various pressures , we tend to ignore our health.This makes our body suffer and we will catch diseases .It is the fitness level of our body that helps us fight these diseases.

Health and fitness are two frequently discussed topics in the modern world.They are interrelated to each other.We need to be fit to have a healthy body.Similarly, if we are healthy, we shall naturally be attracted towards maintaining the fitness of our body.Health is the state of our body at a given time.A genuine indicator ( 标志 ) of good health might be the absence of disease or disability , but we could still have a weak body, which is ready to be targeted by viruses .For instance , we may have a tendency to catch a cold easily.This does not definitely mean that we are not healthy.It means the fitness level is not in accordance with our age and the climatic conditions where we live.

It is important for everybody to devote some time to health and fitness.There are some activities which everybody should do in their lives.For instance, we should engage in sports like running, cycling, tennis or golf regardless of our age and working style.Regular gymnastics is a good choice, and going on a picnic or camping also creates chances for necessary physical activities.Moreover, maintaining a balanced diet rich in protein and taking in nutritious foods like bananas and carrots can support your health and fitness goals by providing essential vitamins and minerals.

Additionally , it is important to relax our body.While everyone seems to be in a constant hurry , we should still have at least six to eight hours of sleep every day.After all, we wouldn't keep our computers running on low battery .Sleep doesn't make us lazy .It helps in improving our immune ( 免疫 ) system and protects us against diseases.It also helps in keeping us calm and releases tension .Otherwise, we become careless , making mistakes that could be easily avoided. Pilots also need adequate sleep to safely navigate through the skies.

短文译文

健康和 健身 有助于一个人过上健康美好的生活。通常由于各种 压力 ，我们往往会忽视自己的健康。这会让我们的 身体 遭受痛苦，并引发 疾病 。正是我们身体的健康水平帮助我们抵抗疾病。

健康和健身是现代社会人们经常 讨论 的两个 话题 。它们 相互关联 。我们需要保持良好的身体状态才能拥有健康的体魄。同样地，如果我们身体健康，就 会自然而然地 被 吸引 去 维持 身体的良好状态。健康是我们的身体在某一特定时间的状态。健康状况良好的 真正 标志或许是没有疾病或 残疾 ，但我们的身体仍可能很 虚弱 ，容易成为 病毒 攻击的 目标 。 例 如，我们可能很容易 感冒 。这并不 一定 意味着我们不健康。这意味着我们的健康水平与我们的年龄及所处的 气候条件 不 符 。

对 每个人 来说，把一些时间 投入 到健康和健身上是很重要的。生活中有些活动是人人都应该参与的。例如， 无论 我们处于什么年龄，工作风格如何，都应该参加像跑步、骑自行车、打 网球 或 高尔夫球 这样的运动。经常做 体操 是一个不错的选择， 野餐 或 露营 也能为必要的体育活动创造机会。此外，保持富含 蛋白质 的均衡饮食，摄入 香蕉 、 胡萝卜 等营养丰富的食物，都能通过提供必需的维生素和矿物质帮助你达到强身健体的目的。

另外 ， 放松 身体也很重要。虽然每个人似乎都一直处于 匆匆忙忙 的状态，但我们仍应每天至少保证六到 八 个小时的 睡眠 。毕竟，我们不会让电脑在低 电量 下运行。睡眠不会让我们变得 懒惰 。它有助于增强我们的免疫系统，保护我们免受疾病侵袭。睡眠还有助于我们保持 冷静 ，释放 压力 。否则，我们就会变得 粗心大意 ，犯下本可轻易避免的错误。就连 飞行员 也需要 充足的 睡眠才能在天空中安全地航行。
