Who is a genius ? This question has greatly interested humankind for centuries .

Let's state clearly : Einstein was a genius.His face is almost the international symbol for genius.But we want to go beyond one man and explore the nature of genius itself.Why is it that some people are so much more intelligent or creative than the rest of us? And who are they?

In the sciences and arts , those praised as geniuses were most often white men, of European racial origin. Perhaps this is not a surprise.It's said that history is written by the victors, and those victors set the standards for admission to the genius club.When contributions were made by geniuses outside the club—women, or people of a different color or belief—they were unacknowledged and rejected by others.Marie Curie, known for her research on radium and radiation , overcame significant challenges related to her gender.Despite societal norms valuing“ Sir ”over“ Madam ”，Curie's success shows how talent and perseverance can break barriers, inspiring future generations in science.

A study recently published by Science found that as young as age six, girls are less likely than boys to say that members of their gender are “really, really smart .”Even worse, the study found that girls act on that belief: Around age six they start to avoid activities said to be for children who are “really, really smart”，while they would probably use this phrase to describe a boy .Can our planet afford to have any great thinkers become discouraged and give up? It doesn't take a genius to know the answer : absolutely not.

Here's the good news .In a wired world with constant global communication , we're all positioned to see flashes of genius wherever they appear.And the more we look, the more we will see that social factors like sex , race , and class do not determine the appearance of genius.As a writer says, future geniuses come from those with“ intelligence , creativity , perseverance , and simple good fortune , who are able to change the world.”

谁是 天才 ？几个 世纪 以来， 人类 一直对这个问题 极为 感兴趣。

让我们 明确 一点：爱因斯坦是个天才。他的 脸几乎 是天才的 国际象征 。但是，我们想超越某一个 人 去探讨天才本身的 本质 。为什么有些人比我们 其他人聪明 得多，或更有创造力？他们又是谁？

在 科学 和 艺术 领域，那些被誉为天才的人往往是欧洲 种族 血统的 白人 男性。 也许 这并不令人惊讶。据说历史是由胜利者书写的，而也是那些胜利者制定了 进入 天才俱乐部的 标准 。当俱乐部外的天才——女性，或有不同 肤色 或信仰的 人 ——做出 贡献 时，他们 不被认可 ，甚至会遭到其他人的 排斥 。玛丽·居里因研究 镭 和 辐射 而闻名，她克服了与自身性别有关的重大挑战。尽管社会规范重“ 先生 ”而轻“ 女士 ”，但居里的成功表明，天赋和毅力可以突破阻碍，激励着科学领域的后辈。

《科学》杂志 最近 发表的一项研究发现，年仅六岁的 女孩 就比 男孩 更不太可能说自己 性别 的人“非常非常 聪明 ”。更糟糕的是，该研究还发现女孩会按照这种观念行事：大约在六岁时，她们就开始避免参与那些被认为是“非常非常聪明”的孩子进行的活动，而她们可能会用这句话描述一个 男孩 。我们的星球能承受得起任何一位伟大的 思想家 感到气馁并放弃吗？即便不是天才也知道 答案 ：绝对不能。
