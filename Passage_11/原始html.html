I'd just arrived at school, ready for another school day.I was reading a book in the classroom when there was an announcement .“ Today at 19:00 there will be auditions ( 试演 ) for a musical .”My friend jumped up in excitement and asked me,“Will you be going tonight , Amy?”“Sure,”I said.I had no interest in drama , but it was OK to try it out because my friends were doing it.

At 19:00, there was a line outside the drama room . Everyone looked energetic , holding their entry forms.I hadn't expected I'd be standing there that morning .But now that I was doing it, I suddenly felt nervous and clenched ( 攥紧 ) my fists .What if my rating was below average? Just then, my friend squeezed my wrist as a little encouragement.

I adjusted my collar and entered the room.The teachers made me say some lines from the musical.They then tested my singing skills and asked what role I wanted to play.The teachers were smiling and praising me.I felt like I had a chance, so I said,“A big role.”They said they'd look into it.I started getting really nervous.What if I didn't get a main role?

Soon , the cast list was posted.My friend checked and came back shouting ,“Amy, you got the main role! Congratulations !”Sure enough, my name was at the top.I just stared at it and started to well up.I was so happy .

After two months, we were all prepared and ready to go on stage in our costumes .And when people started clapping like rolling thunder , that gave me a boost of confidence .As I watched the recording of our performance, I realized how close I came to missing this incredible experience.It stayed with me and made me feel braver .Life isn't about waiting in the back row —I realized that by trying something new, I can have fun—even if it means stepping out of my comfort zone.
