We all grew up hearing people tell us to “go out and get some fresh air.”But is fresh air really as good for you as your mother always said? According to recent studies, everyone should step outside and take a deep breath of fresh air, if the air quality in their camping area is good.

Fresh air cleans our lungs , helping to reduce the symptoms of flu or a persistent cough .If the air you're breathing is clean—which it would be if you're away from the smog of cities—then the air is filled with life-giving , energizing oxygen.If you exercise out of doors , for example, taking a bike ride or enjoying a hike up a mountain, your body will start to breathe more deeply, allowing even more oxygen to get to your muscles and your brain.

Recently, people have begun studying the connection between the natural world and healing ( 治愈 ).Imagine sitting by a fountain , watching a rainbow form after a light drizzle, or simply enjoying the windy weather on a clear day.All across the country, recovery centers have begun building Healing Gardens.In these places, patients can go to be near nature during their recovery.It turns out that just looking at green, growing grass and trees can reduce stress, lower blood pressure, and put people into a better mood. Greenery is good for us. Hospital patients who see tree branches from their window are likely to recover at a faster rate than patients who see buildings or sky instead.Being in nature refreshes us, as we hear the birds singing or spot an eagle flying.It gives us a great feeling of peace .

Another side benefit of getting fresh air is sunlight .While the sun's rays can age and hurt our skin , they also give us beneficial vitamin D, which is crucial for maintaining strong bones .To make sure you get enough vitamin D—but still protect your skin from skin cancer —put on sunscreen right as you head outside.It takes sunscreen about fifteen minutes to start working, and that period is typically enough for your skin to absorb a day's worth of vitamin D.Besides, sunlight has a positive effect on our mood, bringing cheer and helping to ease feelings of sorrow .
