Race walking shares many fitness benefits with running, research shows, while most likely contributing to fewer injuries.It does, however, have its own problem.

Race walkers are highly trained athletes . Tournaments take place year-round ( 全年的 ), from January through December .Enthusiasts from various nationalities participate, competing for gold and silver medals .The colorful flags waving in the wind create a lively setting for the intense races.The longest track and field event at the Summer Olympics is the 50-kilometer race walk , which is about five miles longer than the marathon.The sport's rules require that a race walker's knees stay straight through most of the leg swing and one foot remain in contact with the ground at all times.Therefore, we see athletes waddle ( 蹒跚行走 ) like ducks , kicking up dust along the track.Athletes must negotiate the fine line between maximum speed and maintaining proper formIt's this unique form that makes race walking such an attractive activity, however, says Jaclyn Norberg, an assistant professor of exercise science at Salem State University in Salem, Mass.

Like running, race walking is physically demanding , she says.According to most calculations , race walkers moving at a pace of six miles per hour will burn about 800 calories per hour, which is approximately twice as many as they would burn walking, although fewer than running, which would probably burn about 1,000 or more calories per hour.

However, race walking does not pound the body as much as running does, Dr.Norberg says.According to her research, runners hit the ground with as much as four times their body weight per step, while race walkers, who do not leave the ground, create only about 1.4 times their body weight with each step.

As a result, she says, some of the injuries associated with running, such as runner's knee, are uncommon among race walkers.It seems that there's no need for race walkers to envy runners' freedom of movement.But the sport's unique form does place considerable stress on the ankles and hips , so people with a history of such injuries might want to be cautious in taking up the sport.In fact, anyone wishing to try race walking should probably first consult a coach or experienced racer to learn proper technique, she says.It requires some practice.

短文译文

研究表明，竞 走 与跑步一样，对健康有很多好处，而且竞走导致身体受伤的几率可能会更低。不过，竞走也有其自身的问题。

竞走 运动员 都是 训练有素的 运动员。 锦标赛 全年都有，从 一月 到 十二月 。来自不同 国家 的爱好者都会参加比赛，争夺金 银奖牌 。五颜六色的 旗帜 迎风招展，为激烈的比赛营造出热闹的氛围。夏季 奥运会 上最长的田径 项目 是50公里竞 走 ，比马拉松长大约五 英里 。这项运动的规则要求运动员在 摆动腿 的大部分时间里 膝盖 要保持伸直，并且一只脚始终与地面接触。因此，我们会看到运动员像 鸭子 一样蹒跚前行，在赛道上扬起 尘土 。运动员必须 处理 好最大速度和保持 正确 姿势之间的微妙平衡。不过，马萨诸塞州塞勒姆州立大学运动科学 助理 教授雅克琳·诺伯格说，正是这种独特的姿势让竞走成为一项极具 吸引力的 活动。

她说，和跑步一样，竞走对 体力 的 要求很高 。根据大多数的 计算 结果，以每小时六英里的 速度 竞走的人每小时会消耗大约800 卡路里 的热量，这 大约 是普通行走时消耗热量的 两倍 ，不过比跑步时消耗的热量少，跑步每小时可能会消耗大约1000卡路里或更多的热量。

但是，诺伯格博士说，竞走对身体的 冲击 不像跑步那么大。根据她的研究， 跑步者 每跑一步对地面的冲击力能达到自身体重的四倍，而竞走者无需离开地面，每一步对地面的冲击力仅为自身体重的1.4倍左右。

因此，她说，与跑步 有关的 一些损伤，如膝关节疼痛，在竞走者 中 并 不常见 。看来，竞走者无需 羡慕 跑步者的动作自由了。但这项运动的独特姿势确实会给 脚踝 和 臀部 带来 相当大的 压力，所以有此类伤病史的人在从事这项运动时可能要 谨慎 。她说，事实上， 任何 想尝试竞走的 人 都应该先 咨询教练 或 有经验的参赛者 ，学习正确的技巧。这需要一定的练习。
