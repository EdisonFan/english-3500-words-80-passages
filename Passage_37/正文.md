# Passage 37

If something that you're doing doesn't challenge you, then it doesn't change you. Spoiled by modern civilization, we easily settle into our daily routines. Yet we all need some normal stress in our lives, as the most rewarding experiences often lie beyond the familiar, waiting for pioneers to explore them. Think of comfort zones as a cozy blanket that keeps us warm but also restricts our motions. You can never see any improvement if you stick to it. The willingness to face trials is what successful people share as a common trait, whether they are a respected gentleman or lady, or a badminton champion. Challenge the following limits. It's essential to recognize that personal growth occurs when we dare to step into the distant unknown.

Figure out what you're scared of and do it continuously.

Many people feel dizzy at the thought of change, and that's perfectly natural. This temporary discomfort is a small price to pay for the treasure waiting on the other side. If you're a salesman, and you're scared of talking to people personally or over the phone now, instead of being frightened and thinking you'll fail, spend at least five minutes a day picking up the phone and make a call. Someone may hang up on you, but don't get too disappointed! Eventually, you can look fear in the eye and say,“Go on. I'm not scared!”

Take a class for a hobby you've been wanting to develop.

The journey out of one's comfort zone doesn't require a grand gesture like climbing a tower or becoming an astronomer. Make sure this hobby is not linked to your career; it should help you relax and relieve stress. Some examples might be learning a new language like German or Russian, trying to play the guitar and so on. Apart from helping you challenge yourself, taking a class for your hobby may also give you extra income.

Set aside at least nine minutes a day for physical exercise.

You don't need to go to the gym. A simple nine-minute run around your neighborhood can do wonders for you. Exercise can not only help you maintain your regular weight, but also make you feel better about yourself.

Travel and open yourself up to meeting new people.

Don't just limit yourself to your fellow travelers, and try to connect with the service staff. You never know what kind of people they're going to be. Book a ticket and take a flight to an unfamiliar destination right now.
