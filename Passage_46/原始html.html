Making a meaningful career choice is a crucial decision in life.There are many paths that you can take to contribute positively to the world, such as being a physician , a teacher or a charity worker .However, you don't have to limit yourself to just those choices.To have a lasting and meaningful impact, it's important to consider the problem you expect to overcome.

Define what's personally satisfying .

To ensure success, remember that your career is a marathon and not a short running race.Most people reach peak productivity between the ages of forty and sixty .Thus, there is a difference between a job and a truly meaningful profession .Some may have an appetite for challenge and growth, while others regard security as a key factor.

Define your career impact.

To maximize your career impact, consider the importance of the problem you are addressing .For example, astronauts explore space, contributing to our understanding of the universe; chemists develop new materials that benefit society; professionals in biology help promote sustainable practices.Being a nurse , meanwhile, allows you to provide support to individuals in need.You should also consider your personal fit , which depends on your abilities and career capital—skills, connections, and reputation .

Explore the key categories of meaningful careers.

Before thinking about specific career paths, it's valuable to consider what kinds of careers tend to have the highest impact.Sometimes, it's helpful to talk to an employment agency or a career consultant who can advise you on potential paths and the relevant certificates or qualifications .Remember that many career categories can enable you to make a big contribution to whichever global problems you think are most pressing .

Have a plan B and a plan Z.

List promising alternatives you can switch to if your plan A doesn't work out.If you're ambitious enough, your plan A probably has a good chance of not working out.If you choose a career in a highly competitive field like finance or fashion , it's wise to have an alternative path, such as a role in marketing or design.To avoid unacceptable risks, write a plan Z—how you'll get back on your feet if things go wrong.Exploring a different option will give you more freedom to follow your passions, no matter where they lead.

短文译文

做出一个 有 意义的 职业选择是人生中 至关重要的 决定。你可以通过很多途径为世界做出 积极 贡献，比如成为 医生 、 教师 或慈善 工作者 。但是，你不必拘泥于这些选择。要产生持久且有意义的影响，重要的是考虑你期望克服的问题。

明确 在个人层面上 令人满意的 事物。

为了确保你会成功，要记住职业生涯是一场 马拉松 ，而不是一次短跑赛。大多数人的工作效率会在 四十 到 六十 岁之间达到 顶峰 ，因此一份工作和一个富有意义的 职业 之间存在区别。有些人也许 渴望 挑战和成长，而有些人则将 安全感视为 关键的因素。

明确你的职业影响力。

为了 最大限度地利用 职业的影响力，请考虑你正在 解决 的问题的重要性。例如， 航天员 探索太空，有助于我们了解宇宙； 化学家 开发对社会有益的新材料； 生物学 专业人士帮助推进可持续发展的实践。 护士 则可以为有需要的人提供帮助。你还应该考虑自身的 适配度 ，这取决于你的能力，以及职业资本，包括技能、人脉和 声誉 。

探索有意义职业的主要类别。

在考虑具体的职业道路之前，思考哪些类型的职业往往能产生最大的影响力，这一步是很有价值的。有时，与职业 介绍所 或职业 顾问 交谈会有所帮助，在潜在的职业道路、 相关证书 或 资质 等方面，他们可以为你 提供建议 。请记住，许多职业类别都能让你对 任何 你认为最 紧迫的 全球性问题做出重大贡献。
