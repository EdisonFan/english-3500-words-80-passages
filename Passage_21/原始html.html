扫码跟读

When I was a little girl, I liked drawing, freely and joyously making marks on the walls at home.In primary school , I learned to write using chalk .Writing seemed to be another form of drawing .I shaped individual letters into repeating lines, which were abstract forms, delightful but meaningless patterns .

In secondary school, art was my favorite subject.Since I loved it so much I thought I was good at it. For the art O-level exam I had to present an oil painting .I found it difficult, but still hoped to pass .I failed, with a low grade.I'd been over-confident . Now I'd been declared talentless .

But channels of creativity stayed open elsewhere : I went on writing poems and stories.Still, I went to exhibitions often.I continued my habitual drawing, which I now characterized as childish doodling ( 乱画 ).In my 30s, I made painter friends and learned new ways of looking at art.However, I couldn't let myself have a go at actually doing it.Though these new friends were abstract painters using oil paints , or were printmakers or sculptors , I took oil painting as the taboo ( 禁忌 ) high form I wasn't allowed to practice.

One night , in my early 40s, I dreamed that a big woman in red approached me , handed me a bag of paints, and told me to start painting.The dream felt so authoritative that it shook me.It was a form of energy , giving me back something I'd lost. Accordingly , I started by experimenting with water colors. Finally , I bought some oil paints.

Although I have enjoyed breaking my decades-long taboo about working with oil paints, I have discovered I now prefer chalk and ink .I let my line drawings turn into cartoons I send to friends.It all feels free and easy.Un-anxious.This time around, I can accept my limitations but keep going.

Becoming a successful painter calls for being resolute .I realized I was always afraid of wanting too much.That dream reminded me that those fears and desires could encourage me to take risks and make experiments.

短文译文

当我还 是 个小女孩的时候，我就喜欢画画，在家里的墙壁上 自由 、 愉快地 涂涂画画。上 小学 时，我学会了用 粉笔 写字。写字似乎是另一种形式的 绘画 。我把单个字母塑造 成 重复的线条， 这些线条 具有 抽象的 形式，是 令人愉悦 却 没有意义的图案 。

中学 时，美术是 我 最喜欢的科目。因为我非常喜欢 它 ，所以我以为自己擅长于此。在美术的普通学科水平 考试中 ，我需要提交一幅 油画 。我觉得这很难，但仍然 希望 能考试 及格 。结果我没及格，分数很低。我之前 太过自信 了。 现在 我被 判定 为 没有天赋 。

但是，创造力 的渠道 在 别处 仍 保持 畅通：我 继续 写诗和故事。我依然经常去看 展览 。我保持着画画的 习惯 ，但现在我把这 称为幼稚的 乱画。30多岁时，我结识了一些 画家 朋友， 还 学会了以新的视角看待艺术。然而，我无法 让 自己真正去尝试做这件事。尽管这些新朋友都是使用油画 颜料 的抽象画家， 或者 是 版画家 、 雕塑家 ，但我把油画视为禁忌的高级艺术形式，认为自己不被允许进行尝试。

在40岁 出头 的 一天夜里 ，我梦见一个穿红衣服、身材高大的女人 走近我 ，递给我一袋颜料，让我开始画画。这个梦充满威严，让我震撼不已。它是一种 能量 ，将我曾经失去的某些东西重新还 给 了我。 于是 ，我先 从 尝试水彩画开始。 最后 ，我 买 了一些油画颜料。

虽然 我很 享受 打破几十年来对使用油画颜料的禁忌，但是我发现自己现在更喜欢粉笔和 墨水 。我把线条画变成了 卡通画 ， 寄 给朋友们。这一切都让我感到轻松自由，没有焦虑。这一次，我可以接受自己的 局限 ，但仍会继续坚持下去。

成为一名成功的画家需要有 坚定的 决心。我意识到，我总是 害怕索求太 多。那个梦 提醒 我， 那些恐惧 和欲望可以鼓励我去冒 险 和尝试。
