扫码跟读

Would you BET on the future of this man? He is 53 years old.Most of his adult life has been a losing struggle against debt and misfortune .A war injury has made his left hand stop functioning , and he has often been in prison . Driven by heaven-knows-what motives , he made a resolution to write a book.

The book turns out to be one that has appealed to the world for more than 350 years.That former prisoner was Cervantes, and the book was Don Quixote .And the story poses an interesting question: why do some people discover new vitality and creativity until the end of their days, while others go to seed long before?

We've all known people who run out of steam before they reach life's halfway mark.I'm not talking about those who fail to get to the top.We can't all get there.I'm talking about people who have stopped learning or growing because they have adopted the fixed attitudes and opinions that all too often come with the passing years.

Most of us, in fact, progressively narrow the variety of our lives.We succeed in our field of specialization and then become trapped in it.Nothing surprises us.We lose our sense of wonder.But, if we are willing to learn, the opportunities are everywhere.

The things we learn in maturity seldom involve information and skills.We learn to bear with the things we can't change.We learn to avoid self-pity.We learn that however much we try to please , some people are never going to love us—an idea that troubles at first but is eventually relaxing .

With high motivation and enthusiasm , we can keep on learning.Then we will know how important it is to have meaning in our life.However, we can achieve meaning only if we have made a commitment to something larger than our own little egos ( 自我 ), whether to loved ones, to fellow humans, to work, or to some moral concept.

Many of us equate ( 使等同 )“commitment” with such “caring” occupations as teaching and nursing .But doing any ordinary job as well as one can is in itself an admirable commitment.People who work toward such excellence whether they are driving a truck , or running a shop make the world better just by being the kind of people they are.They've learned life's most valuable lesson .

你会 下赌注 在这个人的未来上吗？他53岁了，成年后的大部分时间都在 与债务 和 不幸 进行一场失败的 斗争 。战争中受的 伤 使他的 左 手失去了 功能 ，并且他还经常入 狱 。在不知是什么 动机 的 驱使 下，他下定 决心 要写一本书。

事实证明，这本书350多年来一直 吸引 着全世界的读者。这位前 囚犯 就是塞万提斯，而这本书就是《堂吉诃德》。这个故事引出了一个有趣的问题：为什么有些人直到生命的尽头还能发现新的 活力 和创造力，而另一些人却早早地就萎靡不振了呢？

我们都知道，有些人还没到人生半程就丧失了 热情 。我并不是在说那些 没能 登上顶峰的人。我们不可能都做到这一点。我所说的是那些因为随着年龄的增长而 形成 了固定的 态度 和 观点 ，从而停止学习和成长的人。

事实上，我们大多数人都在 逐渐缩小 自己生活的 多样性 。我们在自己的 专业领域取得成功 ，然后便 受困 其中。没有什么能让我们感到惊讶。我们失去了好奇心。但是，如果我们 愿意 学习， 机会 是无处不在的。
