Passage 25

扫码跟读

I have a special place in my heart for libraries .I was always an enthusiastic reader , sometimes reading up to three books a day as a child.Stories were like air to me and while other kids played ball or went to parties, I lived out adventures through the books I checked out from the library, lost among the pages of stories that took me to far-off ( 遥远的 ) places like the equator or ancient palaces .The scent of old paper and the quiet whisper of turning pages were my favorite.

My first job was working at the Ukiah Library when I was 16 years old.It was a dream job and I did everything from shelving books to reading to the children for story time.When the bell above the library entrance rang , it was the start of another day filled with discovery.

As I grew older and became a mother, the library took on an added meaning in my life.I had several children and books were our main source of entertainment .It was a big deal for us to load up and go to the local library.We'd pack a picnic with hamburgers , dumplings , or pies , and stroll downtown to our favorite library branch, where my kids could pick out books to read or books they wanted me to read to them .

After having the picnic food for lunch, I read stories about dragons , whales , and adventures.I used different voices, as though I were acting out the stories with my voice and they loved it! We would draw scenes from stories with crayons , making it a special time to bond with my children and it filled them with the wonderment of books.

Now, I see my children taking their children to the library and I love that the excitement of going to the library lives on from generation to generation.

As a novelist , I've found a new relationship with libraries.I encourage readers to go to their local library when their budget is limited and can't afford to purchase a book.I see libraries as a safe haven ( 避风港 ) for readers and writers, a bridge that helps put together a reader with a book.My favorite library is a noble building with gray stone walls and tall columns that reach toward the sky.I remember the kind librarian , Mrs. Brown, who has dedicated nineteen years of her life to preserving the treasures within its walls.She organized gatherings at the library's grand hall , which attracted even the local council workers.Libraries, in their own way, help fight book piracy ( 盗版行为 ) and I think all writers should support libraries in a significant way when they can.Encourage readers to use the library. Share library announcements on your website and social media. Frequent them and talk about them when you can.

图书馆 在 我 心中 有 着特殊的位置。我一直是个 满怀热情的读者 ，小时候 有时 一天能读多达三本书。故事对我来说就像 空气 一样不可或缺，当其他孩子打 球 或参加派对时，我正在从图书馆借来的书中体验着各种 冒险 ，沉浸在书页中那些把我带到遥远地方的故事里，比如 赤道 或古老的 宫殿 。旧纸张散发的气味和翻动书页时轻轻的 沙沙声 都是我的最爱。

我的第一份工作是在尤凯亚图书馆上班，当时我16岁。那是我 梦寐以求的 工作，从图书 上架 到给孩子们讲故事，我做了 所有的事 。当图书馆入口处上方的 铃 声 响起 时，充满探索的另一天就开始了。

随着年岁的增长，我成了一位母亲，图书馆在我的生活中有了 新的 意义。我有几个孩子，书籍是 我们 主要的 娱乐 来源。对我们来说， 装 好东西去 当地 图书馆是一件大事。我们会 带上 一份有 汉堡包 、 饺子 ，或 馅饼 的野餐，然后漫步到 市中心 那家我们最喜欢的图书馆分馆。在那里，我的孩子们 可以 挑选想读的书，或者想让我读给 他们 听的书。
