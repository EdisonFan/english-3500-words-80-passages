扫码跟读

Ralph Emerson once said that the purpose of life is not to be happy, but to be useful, to be loving , to make some difference in the world.While we appreciate such words of wisdom , we rarely try to follow them in our lives.

Most people prefer to live a good life themselves , ignoring their responsibilities to the world.This narrow perception of a good life may provide short-term benefits, but is sure to lead to long-term harm and suffering .A good life based on comfort and luxury may eventually lead to more pain because we spoil our health and even our character, principles , ideals , and relationships.

What then, is the secret of a good life? A good life is a process, not a state of being: a direction, not a destination.We have to earn a good life by first serving others without any expectation in return because their happiness is the very source of our own happiness.More importantly , we must know ourselves inside out.Only when we examine ourselves deeply can we discover our abilities and recognize our limitations, and then work accordingly to create a better world.

The first requirement for a good life is having a loving heart.When we do certain right things merely as a duty , we find our job so tiresome that we'll soon burn out.However, when we do that same job out of love, we not only enjoy what we do, but also do it with an effortless feeling.

However, love alone is insufficient to lead a good life.Love sometimes blinds us to reality . Consequently , our good intentions may not lead to good results.To achieve desired outcomes, those who want to do good to others also need to equip themselves with accurate world knowledge. False knowledge is more dangerous than ignorance .If love is the engine of a car , knowledge is the steering wheel .If the engine lacks power, the car can't move; if the driver loses control of the steering, a road accident probably occurs.Only with love in our heart and the right knowledge in our mind can we lead a good life.

With love and knowledge, we go all out to create a better world by doing good to others.When we see the impact of our good work on the world, we give meaning to our life and earn lasting joy and happiness.

短文译文

拉尔夫·爱默生曾经说过，生活的目的不是为了快乐，而是为了成为有用的人， 富有爱心 ，并在这个世界上留下一些影响。虽然 我们 欣赏这样的 智慧 之言，却 很少 在生活中去践行它们。

大多数人更喜欢 自己 过好日子，而忽视了对世界的责任。这种对美好生活的狭隘 看法 可能会带来 短期 利益，但必然导致 长期的伤害 和 痛苦 。建立在舒适和 奢华基础 上的美好生活，最终可能会引发更多的 痛苦 ，因为我们 损害 了自己的 健康 ，甚至是自己的品格、 原则 、 理想 和人际关系。

那么，美好生活的 秘诀 是什么呢？美好的生活是一个过程，而不是一种状态：它是一个方向，而不是一个终点。我们首先必须要不 求 回报地为他人服务，以此来赢得美好的生活，因为他人的 幸福 正是我们自身幸福的源泉。更 重要 的是，我们 必须彻底 了解 自己 。我们只有 深刻地 审视自己，才能发现自己的能力，认识到自身的局限，并据此努力创造一个更美好的世界。

美好生活的首要 条件 是拥有一颗充满爱的心。当我们仅仅把某些正确的事当作 责任 去做时，会发现我们的工作是如此 乏味 ，以至于我们很快就会 精疲力竭 。然而，当我们出于爱去做同样的事情时，我们不仅会乐在其中，还会有一种 毫不费力的 感觉。
