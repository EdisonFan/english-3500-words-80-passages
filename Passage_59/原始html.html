Almost all animals have distinct vocals （声音） that they rely on to either ask for help, scare away any dangerous animals or look for shelter .When a dog barks loudly, it announces that something is wrong or that it needs help. Elephants would use calls in a low frequency to keep the group together, especially when they are in danger .A male bird may sing to attract a mate, trying to convince a female that it is suitable as a partner .

But cats are creatures who possess special vocalization （发声） skills.They are able to have entire conversations with humans using meows and you're able to interpret them.If a pet cat is hungry, it will sit near its food bowl and keep meowing to attract attention.When a cat feels threatened , it might let out an aggressive hiss that serves as a warning .Purring is a way for the cat to show that it feels secure .However, when a cat is looking for affection , it tends to produce stretched and soft meows.Meowing starts as soon as a baby cat is brought to life and is used to get the mother's attention and be fed .

Cats have many heightened senses, but their sense of smell is quite impressive .This ability helps them hunt in the wild.They will sniff out specific areas before they choose a place as their territory .However, another way the cats are able to distinguish between situations is by looking for familiar smells.Your cat will likely smell your face or clothes and store the smell in its memory and use it to recognize you in the future.Therefore, they rub their face against your legs to attach their scent to you.Their scent acts like a form of advertisement , telling other animals that they are familiar with you.

Dogs are known for their impressive fetching habit, but cats take this behavior up a notch .Many cats will find random objects outside and leave them alongside their owners as gifts.This is a very old habit that's been present in all kinds of predators （食肉动物） .Cats bring gifts for their owners to show they love them or to display their authority over the home.These adorable little hunters are just doing something that has been in their nature since the beginning of time. Altogether , cats have unique ways to show their feelings.Just go along with it!

几乎所有动物都有独特的叫声，它们依靠这些叫声来寻求帮助， 吓 跑任何危险的动物或寻求 躲避处 。当一只狗大声吠叫时，它是在 表示 有情况不对劲，或者它需要帮助。 大象 会用低 频 的叫声让象群保持在一起，尤其是在遇到 危险 的时候。雄鸟会 唱歌 来吸引配偶，试图 让雌鸟相信 它是 合适的伴侣 。

然而， 猫 是拥有特殊发声技巧的动物。它们能够通过 喵喵的叫声 与人类进行完整的交流，而人们也能 解读 这些叫声。如果一只 宠物 猫饿了，它就会坐在自己的食 碗 附近不停地喵喵叫以引起注意。当一只猫感受到 威胁 时，它可能会发出具有攻击性的 嘶嘶声 以示 警告 。发出呼噜声是猫表达 安全 感的一种方式。然而，当一只猫想要得到 爱抚 时，它往往会发出 拉长 且轻柔的喵喵声。猫宝宝一出生就开始喵喵叫，并用它来引起猫妈妈的注意，好让自己得到 喂食 。
