# Passage 76

Given the astonishing potential of AI to transform our lives, from transport to healthcare (医疗保健), we all need to take action to deal with our AI-powered future, and this is where AI by Design: A Plan for Living with Artificial Intelligence comes in. This absorbing book by the British tech celebrity Catriona Campbell presents a civil discussion on artificial intelligence and its impact on society. The book is not just a directory of AI-related concepts, but a map that charts the journey of AI development, from its roots in computer science to its frontier applications.

In the wrong hands, such a book could prove as complicated to process as the computer code that powers AI but, thankfully, Campbell reviews her two decades of professional experience and translates the complex into the understandable. She carves out a clear path for readers to follow, writing from the practical angle of a business person rather than as an academic, providing a comprehensive summary in a polite yet frank tone, which is highly accessible and informative.

As we soon come to learn from AI by Design, AI is already super-smart and will become more capable, moving from the current generation of "narrow AI" to Artificial General Intelligence. From there, Campbell comes up with a timely forecast of Artificial Dominant Intelligence. This is why Campbell has set out to raise awareness of AI and its future now—several decades before these developments are expected to take place. She says it is essential that we keep control of artificial intelligence, or risk being sidelined, because the Go-playing AlphaGo already has an advantage over human players.

Campbell's point is to wake up those responsible for AI—the technology companies and world leaders—so they are on the same page as all the experts currently proceeding with it. She explains we are at a "tipping point" in history and must act now to prevent the extinction of humanity. We need to consider how we want our future with AI to pan out. Such structured thinking, followed by legal regulation and proper instruction, will help defend human rights in an AI-driven world.

AI will affect us all, and if you only read one book on the subject, this is it.
