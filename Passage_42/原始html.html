扫码跟读

The elderly residents in care homes in Britain are being given hens to look after to stop them feeling lonely .The initiative has been applauded as a creative cure for elderly loneliness.It was dreamed up by a local charity to reduce loneliness and improve senior citizens ’well-being.It is also being used to help patients suffering from dementia , a serious illness of the mind.Staff in care homes have reported a reduction in the use of medicine where hens are kept.

For elderly individuals who may struggle with the physical demands of housework , caring for hens offers a way to engage in something meaningful.The interactions with hens are simple pleasures that can bring a smile to residents who may have withdrawn from social activities, especially when their family members live abroad or have busy schedules.

“I like the project a lot.I am down there in my wheelchair in the morning letting the hens out and down there again at night to see that they've gone to bed.I have a stomachache sometimes, but looking after those hens helps me forget the ache .I'm not just sitting around feeling sorry for myself anymore.I owe it to this project for breaking the cycle .”

“It's good to have a different focus.People have been bringing their children in to see the hens, and residents come and sit outside to watch them.It brings me a sense of pride and dignity .I'm grateful for that.”

There are now 700 elderly people looking after hens in 20 care homes in the North East, and the welfare center has been given funds and other financial support to roll it out.

Wendy Wilson, extra care manager at 60 Penfold Street , one of the first to embark on the project, said:“Residents really welcome the idea of the project and the creative sessions .We are looking forward to the benefits and fun the project can bring to people here .”

Lynn Lewis, director of Notting Hill Pathways , said:“We are glad to be taking part in the project.It will really help connect our residents through a shared interest and creative activities.”

住在 英国疗养 院的 老年居民 被分配了照顾 母鸡 的任务，以消除他们的 孤独 感。这一 举措 一直备受 称赞 ，被认为是缓解老年人孤独的创意 疗法 。这是当地一家 慈善机构 构想出来的，意在减少 老年人 的孤独感，提高他们的幸福感。这种方法还被用于帮助 痴呆 （一种严重的精神 疾病 ）患者。疗养院的工作人员 报告 说，在养母鸡的地方，药物使用量有所减少。

对于那些可能在应对 家务劳动 的体力要求方面存在困难的老年人来说，照顾母鸡为他们提供了一种参与有意义活动的方式。与母鸡的互动是简单的乐趣，可以给那些可能已经 脱离 社交活动的居民带来笑容， 尤其 是当他们的家人住 在国外 或日程繁忙时。

“我非常喜欢这个项目。早上我坐着 轮椅 过去，把母鸡放出来，晚上再过去看看它们是不是都进窝睡觉了。我有时会 胃痛 ，但照顾这些母鸡让我忘记了 疼痛 。我不再只是闲坐着自 怨 自艾。我应该 感谢 这个项目打破了这个 循环 。”
