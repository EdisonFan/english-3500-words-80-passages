Passage 7

扫码跟读

If the only reading you ever do is the novel or magazine , the speed at which you read— probably does not matter very much.But if you have to read a great deal for study, you will appreciate the benefits of being able to read more quickly .Here are some tips to help improve your reading speed.

Before you open the book , make sure that you are comfortable.You need a seat which supports your back and the book should be at the right distance from your eyes .Keep the room cool rather than warm to avoid feeling sleepy .

Spend a few minutes looking through the book.Look at the table of contents, the preface , the chapter headings , etc.This will help you to decide whether you really need to read the whole book or only certain parts of it.Ten minutes spent in this way could save you quite a lot of time in the long run .

If you decide that you need to read the whole book, decide how much you can read at a time .This depends on the type of book you are reading.A history book, which may contain the facts in story form, will be easier to read than one dealing with scientific subjects .In the former case , you may be able to read a chapter.In the latter , you may only be able to read one page.

Always keep a pencil and paper beside you.Make a note of any page that is of special importance .Note also the facts important for your purpose as well as anything that leads you to further research.You don't have to write these things in detail .It is enough to write down the page number and one or two words as a reminder.

短文译文

如果你 一直 以来 只 读小说或 杂志 的话，那阅读 速度可能 并不那么 重要 。但是，如果你必须为了学习而大 量 阅读，你就会 意识到能够快速 阅读的好处。以下是一些帮助你 提高 阅读速度的小技巧。

在 你 打开书之前 ， 确保 自己处于舒适的状态。你需要一个能 支撑 背部的 座位 ，并且书与 眼睛 的 距离 要适中。室内不要太 暖和 ， 凉爽 一点更好，以 免 感到 困倦 。

先花几 分钟 时间浏览一下这本书。看看目录、 前言 、 章节标题 等。这将帮助你决定是否 真的 需要阅读整本书，还是只需阅读其中的某些部分。从长远来看，花十分钟这样做可以为你节省大量的时间。

如果你决定需要阅读整本书，确定一下你一 次 能读多少内容。这取决于你阅读的书籍 类型 。 历史 类书籍可能 包含 以 故事 的形式讲述的事实，因此比探讨 科学题材 的书籍更容易阅读。在 前一种情况 下，你或许能够读完一章。而对于 后者 ，你可能只能读完一页。
