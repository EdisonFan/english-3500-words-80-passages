If you ever get the impression that your dog is so clever that it can“ tell ”whether you look content or annoyed , you may be onto something.They bark when they sense your anger or wag （摇动） their tails when they see your smile.Dogs may indeed be able to distinguish between happy and angry human faces, according to a new study.In this study, researchers claimed that dogs can distinguish between human facial expressions, just as a son learns to understand his father's emotions.

Researchers trained a group of 11 dogs in a laboratory to distinguish between images of the same person making either a happy or an angry face.During the training phase , each dog was shown only the upper half or the lower half of the person's face. Afterward , the researchers tested the dogs' ability to distinguish between human facial expressions by showing them the other half of the person's face in images totally different from the ones used in training.The dogs were able to pick the annoyed or pleasant face by pressing their noses against the image to indicate their choice, performing better than random chance would predict.

The study provided insights into how dogs apply what they learned about human faces during training to new faces in the testing stage.“We can rule out that the dogs simply distinguish between the pictures based on a simple cue , such as the sight of teeth ,”said study author Corsin Müller.“Instead, our results suggest that the successful dogs realized that a smiling mouth means the same thing as smiling eyes, and the same rule applies to an angry mouth having the same meaning as angry eyes.”

“With our study, we think we can now confidently conclude that at least some dogs can distinguish human facial expressions,”Müller told Live Science .

At this point, it is not clear why dogs seem to have the ability to recognize different facial expressions.“To us, the most reliable explanation appears to be that the basis lies in their living with humans, which gives them a lot of exposure to human facial expressions.”Muller said.It might be a result of careful adaptation over thousands of years and it may be one of the reasons why they play a role in the army and work with policemen .

如果你曾有过这样的 感想 ，即你的狗非常 聪明 ，能够“ 分辨 ”你的表情是 满意 还是 恼怒 ，那你可能有了些发现。它们会在感觉到你的 怒火 时 吠叫 ，或在看到你微笑时摇动 尾巴 。根据一项新的研究，狗也许确实能分辨出人类高兴和 生气 的表情。在这项研究中，研究人员 称 ，狗可以分辨人类的面部表情，就如同一个 儿子 学会理解他父亲的情绪一样。

研究人员在 实验室 里训练了11只狗，让它们分辨同一个人高兴或生气时的面部 图片 。在训练 阶段 ，每只狗只能看到人脸的 上 半部分或 下 半部分。 之后 ，研究人员向狗展示了与训练时完全不同的图片中人脸的另一半，以此测试狗分辨人类 面部表情 的能力。这些狗能够通过将鼻子 贴 在图片上来表明自己的选择，从而挑选出恼怒或 愉快的 表情，它们的表现比随机概率所预测的更好。

这项研究让我们 了解 到，狗是如何将训练中学到的关于人类面孔的知识应用于在 测试 阶段中出现的新面孔的。研究报告的 作者 科辛·穆勒说：“我们可以 排除 狗仅仅是根据简单的 线索 （如看到 牙齿 ）来区分图片的可能性。相反，我们的结果表明，成功完成测试的狗意识到，微笑的 嘴 与微笑的眼睛所传达的含义是一样的，同样的规则也适用于愤怒的嘴与愤怒的眼睛，它们传达的含义也是相同的。”
